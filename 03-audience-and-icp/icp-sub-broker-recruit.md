# ICP — the sub-broker recruit

> **Tier note:** `inferred` from the `/join-us/` page. Commercial terms are unknown — see
> [sub-broker-offer](../02-offer-and-lending/sub-broker-offer.md).

## Who they are, per the published criteria

> brokers with at least **two years of brokerage or banking experience**, and the hustle and
> drive to grow their own business.

Two distinct people fit that:

**The isolated solo broker.** Two to eight years in, writing a reasonable book alone. Doing
their own marketing, own processing, own compliance, own everything. Competent and tired.
The page speaks directly to them: *"Running your own show can be rewarding, but it can also
be lonely."*

**The banker going independent.** Coming out of a lender's mobile-lending or branch channel
with credit knowledge, some relationships, and no infrastructure. Jessica's own background —
Commonwealth Bank — is this exact path, which makes her a credible proof point.

## The job to be done

**"Give me the parts of a team I can't build alone, without taking my business off me."**

The tension the page names precisely: *"You keep your independence and your own business, but
you gain a team, a brand, and the backing."*

## The fears that block the decision

1. **"I'll lose control of my clients."** The deepest fear. Whose book is it?
2. **"The split won't be worth it."** Unanswerable while the split is unpublished.
3. **"It's a lead-generation trap."** Many recruitment offers dress up a lead-buying scheme.
   The page is honest here — *"generate your own leads"* — which is a credibility asset.
4. **"I'll have to change aggregator."** Real switching cost, unaddressed on the page.
5. **"Will I actually get support, or just a logo?"**

## What converts them

- **The loneliness angle.** It is the emotionally true one and nobody else uses it.
- **Evidence the infrastructure is real** — the seven award badges, the operations stack,
  a marketing person on staff. Awards persuade brokers far more than borrowers.
- **Kaiden as proof of development.** A brokerage that took a teenager through Cert IV into
  broking demonstrably invests in people.
- **A conversation, not an application.** The page gets this right: *"We're always happy to chat."*

## Where they are

LinkedIn. Aggregator PD days and conferences. Industry press —
The Adviser, Australian Broker, MPA. Facebook broker groups. Award shortlists.

Small, enumerable, reachable. **This is the one audience where genuine outbound works**,
because there are thousands of them nationally, not millions.

## Blockers before any campaign runs

The four unpublished commercial facts — split, leads, costs, aggregator arrangement — must be
answered by Karlie first. A recruitment campaign that cannot answer "what's the split?" wastes
the traffic it earns.
