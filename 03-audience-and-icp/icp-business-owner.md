# ICP — the business owner

> **Tier note:** `inferred`. Reasoned from published copy.

**The most under-marketed audience relative to capability.** Commercial and asset finance sit
first and second in the homepage's own solutions ordering, and Karlie's published title is
*Commercial & Residential Broker* — but essentially all consumer-facing marketing energy goes
to residential.

## Who they are

35–60. Owner-operator or director of an SME — trades, professional services, medical and
dental practices, retail, transport, light manufacturing. Between roughly 2 and 50 staff.
Often already a home-loan client, or someone whose home loan we could also hold.

The blog already names a specific vertical: *"Using asset finance for your medical or dental
practice"*.

## Trigger events

- Needing equipment or a vehicle now, with cash tied up
- **Payday Super from 1 July 2026** squeezing payroll cash flow — dated, universal, specific
- An ATO obligation they cannot fund from operations
- A lease ending and a decision about buying premises
- A growth opportunity, a large order, or a new contract
- Being declined by their own bank, or handled by someone who does not understand the business

## The job to be done

**"Get me the money without turning it into a second job."**

Business owners are time-poor in a way consumers are not. Every document request is a cost.
The winning promise is not a rate — it is *"tell me what you need once, and I'll handle the rest."*

## The fears that block the decision

1. **"My financials aren't tidy enough."** Extremely common, rarely fatal — the site notes
   low-doc and alternative verification exist.
2. **"I'll have to put my house up."** Security is the emotional core of business lending.
   The site answers it honestly: *"Not always. Some lenders offer unsecured business loans."*
3. **"It'll take too long and I'll lose the opportunity."**
4. **"The bank will say no and it'll show up on my file."**
5. **"Nobody understands my industry."**

## The strongest available positioning

The business's own blog wrote it:

> SMEs are increasingly looking for a broker who can help them navigate **all** their capital
> needs… one adviser who understands the business deeply and can recommend the right mix of
> products is more valuable than multiple disconnected contacts.

The Loans Suite can make this claim honestly — home, commercial, business, asset and SMSF
under one principal. **A business owner's home loan, their premises and their fleet, handled
by one person who already knows their numbers.** Almost no residential brokerage can say that,
and almost no commercial broker will also do the family home.

## What converts them

- **A dated, external trigger** — Payday Super is the best one available.
- **Blunt, short copy.** No hand-holding. This audience wants the answer.
- **Proof of complexity handled** — which the business does not currently publish. This is a
  case-study gap. See [what-we-cannot-claim](../05-proof-and-evidence/what-we-cannot-claim.md).
- **A referral from their accountant** — which is why the accountant partnerships matter.

## Disqualifiers

Pre-revenue startups with no trading history and no security. Anyone seeking finance to pay
out a distressed position without a plan — that is a restructuring conversation.
