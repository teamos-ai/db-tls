# ICP — the first home buyer

> **Tier note:** `inferred`. This profile is reasoned from the business's published copy and
> the tools it built, not from a real prospect's mouth. It is good enough to shape strategy
> and internal drafts. **Every verbatim in the "how they talk" section below is the
> business's language, not a customer's.** Closing this gap — recording actual discovery
> calls — is the highest-leverage item on the
> [enrichment roadmap](../00-start-here/ENRICHMENT-ROADMAP.md).

## Who they are

25–40. Renting, often as a couple, sometimes with a parent guarantor in the background.
Saving a deposit and unsure whether it is enough. Concentrated around the two office
footprints — Moreton Bay and North Brisbane in QLD, western Sydney in NSW — where entry-level
stock is still reachable. Median Redcliffe house price was around $950,000 in early 2026,
which frames the deposit problem precisely.

## Trigger events

- Rent increase or a lease ending
- A friend or sibling buying, making it feel possible
- Being told a deposit amount by a bank and not believing it
- A pay rise or a new job that changes the maths
- Hearing about a government scheme and not knowing if it applies
- A parent offering to help and nobody knowing how that works mechanically

## The job to be done

**"Tell me whether this is even possible, without making me feel stupid for asking."**

Not "find me a loan". They do not yet believe they are a buyer. The job is converting a
vague hope into a concrete number and a next step.

## The fears that block the decision

1. **"We don't have enough deposit."** The 20% figure is lodged in their head as a rule.
2. **"We'll get rejected."** Rejection feels like a permanent verdict, not a data point.
3. **"We'll overpay / buy the wrong thing."** — the site's own blog names this: FOOP,
   Fear of Over-Paying, alongside FOMO and FOBO.
4. **"There's a cost we don't know about."** Stamp duty, LMI, legals — the unknown-unknowns.
5. **"A broker is going to try to sell me something."** The zero-cost model answers this
   completely and is under-used at this stage of the funnel.

## What they search and ask

"how much deposit do I need" · "first home buyer grant [state]" · "how much can I borrow" ·
"stamp duty calculator" · "do I qualify for the 5% deposit scheme" · "is it better to rent or buy"

Note that three of the seven calculators — stamp duty, borrowing power, and to a degree
repayments — exist to answer exactly these searches. **The tools are already aligned to this
ICP; the campaigns are not.**

## What converts them

- A **number**, fast, with no obligation. Borrowing power and stamp duty are the entry points.
- **Permission to ask a basic question.** The first-home page's *"just real answers"* framing.
- **Naming a scheme they hadn't heard of.** The 5% Deposit Scheme, FHSSS, Help to Buy.
- **A person, not a process.** Kaiden is close to this audience in age and story.

## Disqualifiers

None hard. This audience is slow, low-value per file and high in questions — but it is the
top of a thirty-year relationship and it generates referrals from a cohort all buying at once.

## How they should be spoken to

Simplest language in the database. Short sentences. No jargon without a definition. Never
"leverage", never "solutions", never "optimise". Emily's line from her own bio is the register:
**"Small steps today. Big wins tomorrow."**
