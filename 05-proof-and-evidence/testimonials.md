# Testimonials

**Five testimonials exist. That is the entire body of published social proof.**

> **Permission status is unknown for all five.** They are published on the client's own
> website, which implies consent to that use. It does **not** establish consent to use them
> in advertising, on a landing page, in an email, or in a paid ad — which are different uses
> under both privacy expectations and platform advertising policies.
>
> **Rule until permission is confirmed:** use only in the exact form and attribution already
> published, and only in owned channels. Do not use in paid advertising. Getting written
> permission is a top-three roadmap item.

## Samantha G deserves special handling

It is the only piece of proof in the business that demonstrates something a comparison site
and a bank branch **structurally cannot do**: a named person working overnight to save a
deal after a lender pulled an unconditional approval.

**If one permission is obtained, obtain this one.** With consent it could become a case
study, a video, the anchor of the refinance and complex-borrower argument, and a paid ad.

**Compliance note:** any expansion of this story must avoid implying that The Loans Suite can
reverse a lender's decision, or that this outcome is typical. See
[guardrails](../07-compliance-and-guardrails/guardrails.md).

## Usage rules

1. **Attribution as published** — first name plus last initial. Never enrich with a full name,
   suburb, photo or loan detail.
2. **Verbatim.** Do not edit for grammar, tighten, or combine.
3. **Never construct a testimonial**, composite, or "representative" quote.
4. **Never imply volume.** Five testimonials is not "hundreds of happy clients".
5. **Owned channels only** until permission is confirmed.
6. Note Jaesen J's uses the singular *"The Loan Suite"* — that is their wording. Quote it as
   written; do not correct a customer's words.

## The gap

The business almost certainly has more social proof than this — it has a Google Business
Profile and a Facebook page, and reviews accumulate there by default. **None of it is
captured.** Exporting existing reviews is free, immediate, and would multiply the available
proof several times over.
