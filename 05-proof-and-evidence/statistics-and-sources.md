# Statistics and sources

**Every external number used in any asset must appear in this file first.** If it is not
here, it does not go in. Each entry carries source, period and date.

## Broker market share — the category's headline number

| Figure | **81.0%** of all new residential home loans written by brokers |
|---|---|
| Period | **March 2026 quarter** |
| Source | MFAA Quarterly Market Share Report, data by **Cotality**, commissioned by the Mortgage & Finance Association of Australia |
| Reported | Mortgage Professional Australia, 11 June 2026 |
| URL | https://www.mpamag.com/au/news/general/mortgage-brokers-hit-record-81-market-share-in-australia/578334 |
| Researched | 2026-08-26 |

Supporting detail from the same release:
- **$124.88 billion** in new home loans settled by leading aggregators in the March 2026 quarter
- **76.8%** in the March 2025 quarter — a **4.2 percentage point** year-on-year rise
- Broker share has grown from **55.3%** (March 2018) to **81.0%** (March 2026)
- Australia is one of three countries — with the UK and the Netherlands — where brokers
  facilitate more than 80% of mortgage lending

**Why this matters:** it is the single most useful statistic the business can cite. It makes
the case for using a broker without making any claim about The Loans Suite. Attribute it as
*"MFAA, March 2026 quarter"* every time.

## Rules

1. Nothing gets used unless it is in this file.
2. Every use carries source and period.
3. Re-verify anything regulatory or rate-related **before each publication**, not annually.
4. Never state a market figure as a claim about The Loans Suite's own results.
5. When a figure ages out, move it to "Superseded" rather than deleting it — knowing what was
   true in 2023 is useful.
