# What we cannot claim

**This is the most important file in the folder.** It is the list of numbers that do not
exist. Written down explicitly so that no model — now or in a year — fills the silence with
something plausible.

If a figure is on this list and someone asks for it, the correct answer is
**"that isn't in the database"**, not an estimate.

## No business metrics exist

| Claim | Status |
|---|---|
| Number of clients | **Does not exist.** Never "we've helped hundreds/thousands of Australians" |
| Loans settled — count or dollars | **Does not exist** |
| Trail book size | **Does not exist as a verified figure.** An internal document cites "$50M+" with no source. Unusable |
| Approval or success rate | **Does not exist.** Would breach [guardrails](../07-compliance-and-guardrails/guardrails.md) even if it did |
| Average saving from refinancing | **Does not exist.** Never "our clients save on average $X" |
| Years in business / founding year | **Does not exist.** Karlie joined in 2016; the entity's founding date is unpublished |
| Client satisfaction score / NPS | **Does not exist** |
| Google review count or rating | **Not captured** — reviews may exist, but no figure is verified here |
| Number of team members' combined experience | **Contradictory.** The /about page says "decades of combined experience" — that vague phrase is publishable; a number is not |
| Karlie's years of experience | **Contradictory.** Her page says "over 20 years"; Kaiden's page says "over 30". **Unusable until resolved** |
| Turnaround times | **Do not exist** for any stage |
| Number of lenders — **exception** | **"more than 60" IS verified.** See [lender-panel](../02-offer-and-lending/lender-panel.md) |

## No case studies exist

Zero written case studies. No before-and-after. No named or de-identified client stories
beyond the five carousel testimonials.

**Do not construct one**, even de-identified, even labelled "illustrative". A constructed
case study on a regulated credit product is an invented claim.

## No proof of specific outcomes

- No evidence of a specific rate secured for anyone
- No evidence of a deal approved after a decline
- No evidence of a savings figure
- No lender testimonial or BDM reference
- No industry press coverage in the source set

## Contradictions on the live site — resolve before using either side

1. **Karlie's experience:** "over 20 years" vs "over 30 years". Two pages, two numbers.
2. **Mortgage stress threshold:** 35% on `/mortgage-stress-check/`, 30% on
   `/mortgage-stress/`. Use 35%, the newer page. See
   [calculators-and-tools](../02-offer-and-lending/calculators-and-tools.md).
3. **Brand name:** "The Loans Suite" everywhere except `/calculators/mortgage-stress/`, which
   says "The Loan Suite" twice.
4. **Broker market share:** the site's 2023 blog says brokers wrote **69.6%** of new
   residential loans. The current figure is **81.0%** (MFAA/Cotality, March 2026 quarter).
   The blog is not wrong for its date, but must never be cited as current. See
   [statistics-and-sources](statistics-and-sources.md).

## Claims belonging to other businesses — never adopt

The partner pages carry the partners' own claims. They are **theirs**, and require
attribution if used at all:

- *"Product Review winners for Best Credit Repair Company 5 years in a row"* — **Clear Credit
  Solutions**
- *"We've helped remove millions of dollars worth of defaults"* — **Clear Credit Solutions**
- *"Compare up to 6 Australian insurers"* — **Tower Life Insurance**
- *"one of the most respected real estate agencies in southeast Queensland"* — **McGrath North Lakes**
- *"Get Pre-Approved in less than 5 minutes"* — **Enaybl**. Note this contains banned
  language; never reproduce it in our voice.
