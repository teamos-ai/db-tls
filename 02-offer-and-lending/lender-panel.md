# The lender panel

**Single home for the panel figure.** Nothing else in this database restates it.

## The number

> **more than 60 lenders**

Also published as: *"Over 60 banks, credit unions, and non-bank lenders compared to find you
a top rate loan that is suited to your needs."*

**Use "more than 60" or "over 60". Never round to 70, never say "our panel of 60", never
say "all lenders".** The figure appears identically on four pages, which makes it solid.

Context that makes the number land: the panel is set by the aggregator,
**Specialist Finance Group** — see [licensing-and-entity](../01-company/licensing-and-entity.md).

## Named in the privacy policy

The privacy policy names four lenders as parties information may be disclosed to:
**Adelaide Bank, ANZ Bank, Bankwest and Pepper Money.**

These are named in a *disclosure* context, not an endorsement context. Do not repurpose that
list as "our main lenders".

## Copy rules

1. **Never name a lender as a recommendation.** Naming a lender implies preference, which
   conflicts with best interests duty. Logos on a wall are fine; "we'll get you with X" is not.
2. **Never claim a specific lender's policy, rate, or appetite.** We do not speak for lenders.
3. **The safe collective phrasing is the site's own:** *"banks, credit unions and non-bank
   lenders"*. It communicates breadth without naming anyone.
4. **Never say "all lenders" or "the whole market".** 60+ is a panel, not the market.

## Why the panel is a weaker argument than it looks

Every broker claims a panel. Aussie publishes "over 25 leading lenders"; Borro publishes
"30+". At 60+, The Loans Suite is genuinely at the higher end — but the number alone is a
commodity claim that invites a bigger-number response.

**The argument that works is the second half of the site's own sentence:** panel breadth
plus *"Saves you time and money by avoiding endless comparison sites. We do the legwork."*
Access is table stakes. Doing the work is the offer.
