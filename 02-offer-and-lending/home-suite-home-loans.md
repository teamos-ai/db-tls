# Home loans

## The core promise, verbatim

> A home loan should do more than get you approved. It should suit your goals, budget and
> future plans, whether you are buying your first home, refinancing, investing or moving
> into your next property.

> The structure, features and flexibility of the loan can make a big difference to how it
> works for you over time.

## Who it's for

Owner-occupiers buying, upgrading, downsizing or refinancing. Overlaps with
[icp-first-home-buyer](../03-audience-and-icp/icp-first-home-buyer.md) and
[icp-refinancer](../03-audience-and-icp/icp-refinancer.md).

## What's included

- Comparison across **more than 60** banks, credit unions and non-bank lenders
- Borrowing position assessment and pre-approval support
- Paperwork, lender liaison and updates from application to settlement
- **Loan Health Check** — a review of an existing loan against current options
- Post-settlement reviews

## The comparison argument

From `/home-loan-comparison/`, the factors the business says actually matter — a useful
checklist structure for content:

Interest rates and comparison rates · upfront and ongoing fees · fixed, variable and split ·
offset availability · redraw · extra repayment flexibility · loan term and repayment type ·
lender policy and approval requirements · cashback or refinance offers where available ·
how the loan fits long-term plans

> A small difference in rate or loan structure can make a meaningful difference over time.
> However, the cheapest rate is not always the most suitable option if the loan does not
> have the features, flexibility or lender policy you need.

That paragraph is the best compliance-safe articulation of best interests duty on the
whole site. Reuse it.

## Local framing

The pages consistently say **"Sydney, Brisbane and across Australia"** and contrast buyer
types by market — *"A first home buyer in Sydney may need a different loan structure from a
family upgrading in Brisbane or an investor buying interstate."*

## Copy rules

- Never state a rate. Never say "best rate" or "lowest rate".
- "Compare options from more than 60 lenders" — exact figure, no rounding.
- The site's own headline hedge is good practice: *"We make it easy to secure the right loan
  for your goals"* — right loan, not best rate.
