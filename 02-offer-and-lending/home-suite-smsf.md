# SMSF lending

> **The most compliance-sensitive line in the business. Read
> [guardrails](../07-compliance-and-guardrails/guardrails.md) before writing anything here.**

## The regulatory change that reshapes this offer

The site states plainly:

> New SMSF borrowing for residential property is being restricted from **10 August 2026**.
> Existing arrangements may not be affected, but anyone considering a purchase should get
> advice before taking action.

That date has **passed** as at this database's `as_of`. Any asset written now must reflect
that the restriction is in force, not approaching. **Re-verify the current state of the
rules before publishing anything on SMSF** — this is the fastest-decaying fact in the
database.

## What remains, per the site

> SMSF borrowing for **commercial property** may still be available, subject to the fund,
> the property, lender requirements and the correct SMSF loan structure.

The offer has effectively pivoted from residential SMSF to **commercial property SMSF**.
Copy should follow that pivot.

## The mandatory posture

> You will generally need advice from an accountant, financial adviser or SMSF specialist
> before proceeding.

> Yes. SMSF lending involves superannuation law, tax, investment strategy and lending
> requirements, so you should speak with qualified SMSF, tax and financial advice
> professionals before applying.

**Every SMSF asset must carry this deferral.** The Loans Suite handles the *lending* side
and works alongside the client's advisers. It does not advise on superannuation.

The site's own framing of its role is exactly right and should be reused:

> The Loans Suite helps you navigate lender requirements while making sure the lending
> process fits within your broader advice framework.

## The referral-partner angle

SMSF is the strongest **B2B** line in the business, because it cannot proceed without an
accountant or adviser in the room. That makes accountants and financial planners not just
referral sources but **necessary participants**. Two of the twelve referral partners are
accounting firms.

This is the clearest case for a partner-facing campaign rather than a consumer one.
See [icp-referral-partner](../03-audience-and-icp/icp-referral-partner.md).

## Copy rules — absolute

- Never encourage a new residential SMSF purchase.
- Never explain superannuation law, contribution caps, or the sole purpose test.
- Never state a tax outcome.
- Never imply a fund is eligible. Eligibility is a matter for the fund's advisers and the lender.
- Always name the three professional categories: accountant, financial adviser, SMSF specialist.
