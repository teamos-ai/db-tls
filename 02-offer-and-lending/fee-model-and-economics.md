# Fee model and economics

**The single home for how the business gets paid.** Every asset that touches price links
here rather than restating it.

## The published position

Three verbatim statements, repeated across the site:

> Our service is zero-cost. We're paid by the lender once your loan settles so you aren't
> left out of pocket.

> Our services, as a mortgage broker, are complimentary and zero-obligation.

> The best things in life are free…like a consult with a loans suite broker.

## How to say it

**Correct framings:**
- "There's no cost to you — the lender pays us when your loan settles."
- "Complimentary and zero-obligation."
- "You're not left out of pocket."

**Framings to avoid:**
- ~~"Free"~~ as a standalone adjective on the loan. The *service* is at no cost; the *loan*
  has rates and fees set by the lender. "Free home loans" is a compliance problem.
- ~~"We work for you, not the banks"~~ — a category cliché that also invites an awkward
  question about who pays. The site never says it. Neither should we.
- Never explain the commission structure as a *reason to choose a lender*. That inverts
  best interests duty. See [nccp-and-best-interests-duty](../07-compliance-and-guardrails/nccp-and-best-interests-duty.md).

## What is not published — and must not be invented

- **Commission rates.** Upfront and trail percentages are not published. Never state them.
- **Clawback terms.** Lenders claw back commission if a loan is refinanced or discharged
  early, typically within 24 months. This shapes the retention operations
  ([pipelines](../11-operations/pipelines.md)) but is **never client-facing marketing**.
  A client should never learn that our retention call has a commercial motive attached.
- **Trail book size.** An internal document cites a "$50M+ trail book". **This figure has
  no source and must not be used anywhere**, internally or externally, until Karlie confirms it.
  See [what-we-cannot-claim](../05-proof-and-evidence/what-we-cannot-claim.md).
- **Lender fees.** Some lenders charge the customer fees. Aussie's own site carries the
  caveat *"Individual lenders may charge fees to the customer"*. Our copy should not claim
  the whole transaction is free — only our service.

## The second revenue line

Sub-broker arrangements on a **commission split** — brokers running their own business
under The Loans Suite brand. The split percentage is not published.
See [sub-broker-offer](sub-broker-offer.md).

## Why the fee model matters strategically

The zero-cost message does two jobs the business under-uses:

1. **It removes the last objection** before booking. "What does this cost me?" is the
   question sitting behind most unbooked calls, and the answer is unusually good.
2. **It reframes the comparison-site competitor.** A comparison site is also free — but it
   makes the borrower do the work. The site's own line lands this:
   *"Saves you time and money by avoiding endless comparison sites. We do the legwork."*

Lead with the second framing, not the first. "Free" alone is undifferentiated; "free *and*
we do the work" is the actual argument.
