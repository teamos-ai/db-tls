# Personal loans

## The promise, verbatim

> Fast personal loans. The Loans Suite can help you fulfil your wishlist with a personal
> loan suited to your needs.

Purposes named on the page: **a new car, consolidating debts, a wedding, a trip away,
medical needs, or a renovation.**

## Published parameters — use exactly or not at all

| Parameter | Published value |
|---|---|
| Maximum | **$200k** — *"Borrow up to $200k"* |
| Minimum | **$2,000** — *"You need to borrow at least $2,000 with your loan"* |
| Age | 18 years or older |
| Residency | Australian citizen, Permanent Resident, or New Zealand citizen |
| ID | Valid Australian passport or driver's licence |
| Employment | Paid employment, **which may include self-employment** |
| Credit | *"ideally your credit file won't have any defaults, judgments or bankruptcies"* |
| Application | *"Apply in a few minutes over the phone or online"* |

Note the hedge on credit — **"ideally"**, not "must not". Preserve that hedge. It is what
keeps the door open for someone with a blemished file, who is exactly the person the
[Clear Credit Solutions referral partnership](referral-partner-network.md) exists to serve.

## A phrase on this page to never reproduce

The live page contains the line **"Personal loans can be approved"**. It sits directly
against the banned-language rule in
[guardrails](../07-compliance-and-guardrails/guardrails.md). Do not reproduce it in new
copy, and flag it to Karlie as a page to amend.

## Strategic role

Personal loans are the **lowest-value, fastest-cycle** product in the range. Their value
is not margin — it is:

1. **Relationship entry.** A $25k car loan today is a $700k mortgage conversation in two
   years. The homepage testimonial from Mathew B proves the pattern in the business's own
   words: *"home loan and personal loan"*, multiple times.
2. **Reactivation.** An existing mortgage client with a life event — wedding, renovation,
   car — is a warm, low-friction re-engagement.

Do not build a standalone acquisition campaign for personal loans. Build them into
post-settlement nurture and reactivation instead. See
[email-sequences](../08-channels-and-playbooks/email-sequences.md).

## Copy rules

- The word "fast" is the page's own and is acceptable applied to the *application*
  ("apply in a few minutes"). Never apply it to **approval**.
- Debt consolidation is a sensitive purpose. Never suggest it will improve someone's
  position — that is advice. Describe the mechanism, not the outcome.
