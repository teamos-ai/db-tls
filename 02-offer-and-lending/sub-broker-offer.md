# The sub-broker offer

**A second business model hiding on one page.** `/join-us/` recruits brokers, not borrowers.
Different audience, different funnel, different message — and currently no marketing behind it.

## The offer, verbatim

> The Loans Suite is an established, high performing brokerage built on strategy, results and
> genuine client relationships. Now we're looking for driven brokers ready to build their own
> business under a brand that already has the runs on the board.

> We offer **sub broker arrangements with a commission split structure**, giving you the
> freedom to generate your own leads and grow your own business, backed by the systems,
> support and reputation of an established brand.

> You'll get access to our **marketing team, operational support** and a close knit team
> environment, along with **the infrastructure and processes we've spent years refining**.
> You're not starting from scratch. You're building on a strong foundation.

## The hook — and it is a good one

> **Are you a single operator or sick of going it alone?**
>
> Running your own show can be rewarding, but it can also be lonely. If you're a single
> operator broker who is over doing everything yourself with no support, no team and no one
> to bounce ideas off, come and join us.
>
> You keep your independence and your own business, but you gain a team, a brand, and the
> backing to help you grow further and faster than you could on your own.

**"It can also be lonely" is the strongest sentence on the entire website.** It names a real
emotional cost that competitors' recruitment pages never mention — they all lead with splits
and lead flow. This is the angle.

## Who they want

> We're after brokers with at least **two years of brokerage or banking experience**, and the
> hustle and drive to grow their own business.
>
> If you're ambitious, client focused and ready to take ownership of your own success.

Sign-off line: **"Finance. Strategy. Results."**

## What's not published

- **The commission split percentage.** Never state or imply one.
- Whether leads are provided (the copy says *"generate your own leads"* — so, apparently not).
- Whether there is a desk fee, software cost or minimum volume.
- Aggregator arrangements for a sub-broker.

All four are the first questions any experienced broker will ask. **A recruitment campaign
cannot run credibly until Karlie answers them** — this is on the
[enrichment roadmap](../00-start-here/ENRICHMENT-ROADMAP.md).

## Why this deserves real marketing

- **The proof is already there.** Seven award badges are far more persuasive to a broker who
  understands what they mean than to a borrower who does not.
- **The audience is tiny and reachable.** Every mortgage broker in Australia is findable on
  LinkedIn and through aggregator networks. This is the one audience where organic outbound
  genuinely works.
- **Kaiden proves the mentoring claim.** A brokerage that took a 17-year-old through Cert IV
  and into broking has demonstrable evidence it develops people.
- **It compounds.** Each recruited broker is a revenue share on volume they generate, without
  the cost of acquiring their clients.

## Copy rules

- This audience is **not** consumers — but it is still a regulated industry. Never promise
  earnings, lead volume or growth.
- Never state a split.
- **Never disparage aggregators or other brokerages.** The industry is small; the person
  reading is likely at one of them.
- "The runs on the board" is the brand's own idiom and is worth keeping. It is Australian,
  confident, and not corporate.
