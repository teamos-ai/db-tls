# Offer overview

## The architecture

Nine lending lines, in two branded groups. The Suite naming is the brand's own and should
be used — it is more distinctive than "home loans / business loans" and it reinforces the
"financial architects" positioning.

### The Home Suite
| Line | Page | One line |
|---|---|---|
| [Home loans](home-suite-home-loans.md) | `/solutions/the-home-suite/home-loans/` | The core product. Compare 60+ lenders, structure over rate |
| [First home loan](home-suite-first-home.md) | `/first-home-loan/` | Grants, deposits, borrowing power, hand-holding |
| [Investment](home-suite-investment.md) | `/investing-in-property/` | Structure that supports a portfolio strategy |
| [Construction](home-suite-construction.md) | `/construction-loans/` | Staged drawdowns, fixed-price contracts |
| [SMSF](home-suite-smsf.md) | `/smsf-lending/` | **Restricted from 10 Aug 2026 for residential** |
| [Personal loans](home-suite-personal-loans.md) | `/personal-loans/` | Up to $200k, minimum $2,000 |

### The Business Suite
| Line | Page | One line |
|---|---|---|
| [Business loans](business-suite-business-loans.md) | `/business-loans/` | Working capital, growth, stock, expansion |
| [Commercial finance](business-suite-commercial-finance.md) | `/commercial-finance/` | Commercial property, refinance, unlock capital |

**Refinancing** is a major offer with no page of its own — it is treated as a use case
across home loans, investment and the comparison tool. See
[site-map-and-routes](../01-company/site-map-and-routes.md) for the gap.

**Bridging loans** appear as a contact-form option only.

## The homepage's own ordering

The homepage numbers six "Our Solutions" cards in this order — worth knowing, because it
signals what the business wants to sell, which is not what most brokers lead with:

1. **Commercial & Construction Loans**
2. **Asset Finance**
3. Home Loans
4. Loan Refinance
5. Investment Loans
6. SMSF Investment Loans

Commercial and asset finance lead. Home loans are third. That is a deliberate positioning
choice — the business is signalling that it handles complexity, not just residential volume.
Any asset that reduces The Loans Suite to "mortgage broker" is under-selling its own
stated ordering.

## Contact-form taxonomy

The contact form's "What are you looking for help with?" dropdown is the business's own
segmentation of demand. Use these exact labels in any form we build so leads route cleanly:

- Buy a home to live in
- Buy an investment property
- Refinance my home
- Refinance an investment
- Construction Loan
- Bridging Loan
- Asset & Equipment Finance
- SMSF Lending
- Commercial Finance

Note it splits **refinance into owner-occupier and investment**, and that "first home buyer"
is not a separate option — it collapses into "buy a home to live in".

## The offer in one paragraph

> The Loans Suite compares home, investment, construction, SMSF, commercial, business and
> asset finance across more than 60 banks, credit unions and non-bank lenders, structures
> the loan around what you're actually trying to do, manages the application through to
> settlement, and reviews it afterwards to keep it competitive. It costs you nothing — the
> lender pays on settlement.

Every claim in that paragraph traces to a `verified` file. It is safe to use as-is.
