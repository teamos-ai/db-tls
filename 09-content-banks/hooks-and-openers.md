# Bank — hooks and openers

Each row: **the line · when to use it · which pillar · tier.**
Rows tiered `verified` are the client's own published words and are quote-safe.
Rows tiered `inferred` are new, written to the voice rules, and contain no factual claim —
they are safe to use but are our language, not theirs.

## Never use as a hook

Anything implying approval, a rate, a saving, urgency on a credit decision, or knowledge of
the reader's finances. **"Struggling with your repayments?" is banned** — it breaches Meta's
financial-services policy as well as the guardrails. See
[paid-ads](../08-channels-and-playbooks/paid-ads.md).
