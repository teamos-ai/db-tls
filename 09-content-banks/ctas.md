# Bank — calls to action

The business's CTA style is **conversational and first-person from the reader's side** —
*"Help me buy"*, *"Let's talk commercial"*. That is distinctive and worth preserving. Generic
*"Learn More"* / *"Apply Now"* buttons are off-brand.

Match the CTA to the rung of the ladder: **explore → talk → apply.**

## Supporting microcopy

| Line | Where | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| It's zero-obligation, zero cost to chat. | Under a booking CTA | **verified — site** |
| No cost, no obligation, just real answers. | Under a booking CTA | **verified — site** |
| Costs you nothing to find out. | Under a cold CTA | inferred |
| If it's not worth doing, we'll tell you that too. | Under a review CTA | inferred |

That last line is worth using often. Conceding that the answer might be "don't bother" is the
single most trust-building sentence available, and it costs nothing.

## Never use as a CTA

**Get approved · Get pre-approved · See if you qualify · Unlock your best rate · Claim your
savings · Get your free quote** (we don't quote — the lender does) · anything with a
countdown or "limited time".

Note: the live equity calculator's *"Unlock our best home loan rates"* breaches this. Do not
reproduce it. See [calculators-and-tools](../02-offer-and-lending/calculators-and-tools.md).
