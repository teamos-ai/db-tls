# Bank — offer angles

Ways to package what already exists into something a person can say yes to.
**None of these are live.** Each needs Karlie's sign-off before it runs — see
[approval-rules](../07-compliance-and-guardrails/approval-rules.md).

Ranked by leverage: what the business can stand up fastest, against the audience most likely
to convert.

---

## 1 · The Loan Health Check

**Already a named offer on the site with no page, no promise and no funnel attached.**
The single easiest win available.

- **Audience:** refinancers, past clients, the month-10 retention cohort
- **The offer:** a 15-minute review of an existing loan against current options
- **Why it works:** it is specific, time-bounded, low-commitment, already named, and already
  operationalised in Pipeline 6. It also answers the closest competitor's
  "Free Loan Assessment in 30 Minutes" without matching their outcome language
- **Needs:** a page, a booking route, a defined deliverable
- **Never:** promise a saving or a better rate. Promise a **review**

## 2 · The Equity Check

- **Audience:** homeowners 35–60 in Moreton Bay and western Sydney
- **The offer:** estimate your equity in a minute, then find out what's actually usable
- **Why it works:** the calculator already exists and is the best lead-capture asset in the
  business, with intent segmentation built in. Both local markets have had strong growth
- **Needs:** a funnel wrapper. See
  [funnels-and-landing-pages](../08-channels-and-playbooks/funnels-and-landing-pages.md)
- **Never:** reproduce the live page's *"Unlock our best home loan rates"* line

## 3 · Payday Super cash-flow review

- **Audience:** SME employers
- **The offer:** a conversation about funding before super starts leaving weekly from 1 July 2026
- **Why it works:** dated, universal to every Australian employer, and creates a genuine
  working-capital need. Residential-only competitors cannot serve it
- **Needs:** the ATO detail re-verified; a business-finance landing page
- **Never:** give tax or payroll advice. Refer to their accountant

## 4 · The Covid Rate Trap

- **Audience:** anyone who borrowed 2020–2022
- **The offer:** a review of a loan assessed under conditions that no longer exist
- **Why it works:** the business already wrote this post. It is a precise, targetable cohort
  with a genuine, non-obvious reason to act
- **Needs:** the blog post promoted rather than buried

## 5 · Pre-approval expiry rescue

- **Audience:** buyers with a pre-approval nearing 90 days — including competitors' clients
- **The offer:** a second opinion before it lapses
- **Why it works:** a hard deadline, high anxiety, and Pipeline 4 already tracks the timeline
- **Never:** imply we can extend or approve anything

## 6 · The declined second opinion

- **Audience:** anyone recently declined; self-employed, casual, commission, trust structures
- **The offer:** a second look across a 60+ lender panel
- **Why it works:** panel breadth is a real answer here, not a commodity claim. Samantha G's
  testimonial is the proof
- **Never:** imply a decline can be reversed. Say lenders assess differently

## 7 · The build companion

- **Audience:** construction borrowers
- **The offer:** guidance at every drawdown stage, and a post-build revaluation to reduce LVR
- **Why it works:** ten stages over many months is the richest nurture opportunity in the
  business, and the post-build revaluation is concrete value almost nobody mentions

## 8 · Broker recruitment: "you don't have to do it alone"

- **Audience:** solo brokers and bankers going independent
- **The offer:** a conversation about joining
- **Why it works:** the loneliness angle is emotionally true and unused by competitors, and
  the awards do far more work with this audience than with borrowers
- **Blocked until:** Karlie answers split, leads, costs and aggregator arrangement. See
  [sub-broker-offer](../02-offer-and-lending/sub-broker-offer.md)

## 9 · Partner reciprocity

- **Audience:** the four professional categories
- **The offer:** we refer to you, publish you, and keep you updated on your referrals
- **Why it works:** the business already publishes twelve partners — real, demonstrable
  reciprocity almost no competitor offers
- **Blocked until:** consent capture for milestone notifications is designed. See
  [privacy-and-data](../07-compliance-and-guardrails/privacy-and-data.md)

---

## The rule for every angle

**Promise a process, never an outcome.** A review, a conversation, a second look, an estimate
— all safe. A saving, a rate, an approval, a result — none are.
