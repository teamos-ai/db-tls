## Home loans and refinancing

**How much can I borrow?**
That depends on your income, expenses, existing debts and the individual lender's assessment
rules. Our borrowing power calculator gives a rough indication, but it's an estimate only —
actual capacity may be higher or lower after a full lender assessment. *(site)*

**Why is my borrowing power lower than I expected?**
Lenders typically assess applications using a **3% interest rate buffer above the actual
rate**, so they're testing whether you could still afford repayments if rates rose. *(site)*

**Should I just ask my bank for a better rate?**
Yes, ask them — sometimes they'll move. Worth knowing that most lenders price new customers
differently from existing ones, and one lender's answer doesn't tell you what the other sixty
would do.

**Will refinancing hurt my credit score?**
Applying for credit is recorded on your credit file. Whether it affects you depends on your
overall profile and how many applications you make. Nothing touches your credit file until you
decide to apply.

**Is the cheapest rate the best loan?**
Not necessarily. *"The cheapest rate is not always the most suitable option if the loan does
not have the features, flexibility or lender policy you need."* *(site)*

## First home buyers

**How much deposit do I need?**
It depends on the lender, the property and whether you're eligible for any government schemes.
Twenty percent is a common guideline rather than a rule — and since October 2025 the Australian
Government 5% Deposit Scheme has had no place limits and no income caps, so a 5% deposit is a
realistic path for many first home buyers within the property price caps. *(verified 2026-08-27)*

**What government schemes are available?**
Three main ones. The **Australian Government 5% Deposit Scheme** — since 1 October 2025 there
are no place limits and no income caps, with property price caps of $1,000,000 in Brisbane and
Queensland regional centres and $1,500,000 in Sydney and NSW regional centres. The **First Home
Super Saver Scheme** — up to $15,000 a year and $50,000 in total in voluntary super
contributions. And **Help to Buy**, a shared equity scheme open since December 2025, where the
government takes up to 40% of a new home or 30% of an existing one. Eligibility depends on the
scheme rules, your state and your circumstances. *(verified 2026-08-27)*

**Can you help me with Help to Buy?**
Help to Buy has only a small number of participating lenders, and as at July 2026 only one of
them is accessible through brokers. It is worth a conversation about whether the scheme suits
you and what the route to it looks like.

**What's the difference between pre-approval and approval?**
Pre-approval is a lender's conditional indication, subject to conditions and an expiry date —
usually around 90 days. Formal approval comes after the lender has assessed the property and
all conditions are satisfied.

## Investment and equity

**Can I use equity to buy an investment property?**
*"Yes, many property investors use existing equity as part of their deposit or purchase
strategy. The amount available will depend on your property value, current loan balance,
income, expenses and lender requirements."* *(site)*

**Do lenders count rental income?**
*"In many cases, yes. Lenders may include expected or existing rental income, but they often
assess it at a reduced percentage to allow for vacancies, costs and risk."* *(site)*

**Principal and interest, or interest-only?**
*"Both options can suit different strategies. Principal and interest repayments help reduce the
loan balance over time, while interest-only repayments may help with short-term cash flow,
depending on your goals and circumstances."* *(site)*

## Construction

**How does a construction loan work?**
*"A construction loan usually releases funds in stages as each part of the build is completed.
These stages may include slab, frame, lock-up, fixing and completion, depending on the lender
and builder contract."* *(site)*

**Do I pay interest on the whole loan straight away?**
*"Usually, interest is charged only on the amount drawn down during construction. This means
repayments may increase as more funds are released throughout the build."* *(site)*

**Do I need a fixed-price building contract?**
*"Most lenders prefer or require a fixed-price building contract because it helps confirm the
cost of the project. Some lenders may consider other arrangements, but requirements can
vary."* *(site)*

## Business and commercial

**Do I need security for a business loan?**
*"Not always. Some lenders offer unsecured business loans, while others may require residential
property, commercial property, business assets or other forms of security."* *(site)*

**How quickly can business finance be arranged?**
*"Approval timeframes vary depending on the lender, loan type, application strength and
documents required. Some business loans can move in as little as a few hours, while more
complex applications may take longer."* *(site)*

**Can I finance used equipment?**
*"Yes, used equipment may be eligible for finance, but lenders will usually consider the
asset's age, condition, value and expected useful life."* *(site)*

## SMSF

**Can an SMSF still borrow to buy residential property?**
No. New SMSF borrowing to acquire residential property has been banned since **10 August 2026**.
Existing arrangements are grandfathered, and **refinancing an existing residential SMSF loan is
still permitted**. Anyone with an existing arrangement should get advice before acting.
*(verified 2026-08-27 — **re-verify before each publication**)*

**Can I refinance my existing SMSF residential loan?**
Generally yes — refinancing existing arrangements remains permitted on substantially the same
terms. Whether it suits your fund is a question for your accountant, financial adviser or SMSF
specialist alongside the lender.

**Can an SMSF borrow for commercial property?**
Yes — borrowing to acquire **business real property** is still available, subject to the fund,
the property, lender requirements and the correct SMSF loan structure. *(site + verified 2026-08-27)*

**Do I need advice before an SMSF loan?**
*"Yes. SMSF lending involves superannuation law, tax, investment strategy and lending
requirements, so you should speak with qualified SMSF, tax and financial advice professionals
before applying."* *(site)*

## If someone is struggling

If a person indicates they cannot meet their repayments, **stop the sales conversation**.
Point them to their lender's hardship team and the **National Debt Helpline on
1800 007 007** — free financial counselling, which the site itself publishes. See
[disqualifiers](../03-audience-and-icp/disqualifiers.md).
