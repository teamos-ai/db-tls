# The team

Six people. **Use the published titles exactly as below** — several internal documents
carry different, incorrect titles (see the warning at the foot of this file).

## Karlie Scharfenberg — Commercial & Residential Broker

The principal and the brand's face. Phone **0406 870 080**. Booking: Calendly
`loanstratergy/loan-strategy-session` (the typo is in the real URL).

Verbatim, from her page — this is the best single voice sample in the database:

> Karlie began her journey with The Loans Suite in 2016, building on her successful banking
> career to become an award-winning Senior Finance Broker.

> As a "Financial Architect," Karlie's mission is to empower everyday Australians to dream
> big, pay off their family homes, and leverage property investments for passive income,
> ensuring a secure financial future.

> With a Cert IV & Diploma in Finance & Mortgage Broking Management, over 20 years of
> experience, and her role as an NSW Justice of the Peace, Karlie specializes in Residential
> and Commercial Lending, Car & Equipment Finance, and Self-Managed Super Fund Lending.

Credentials that are publishable: **Cert IV and Diploma in Finance & Mortgage Broking
Management**, **NSW Justice of the Peace**, **with The Loans Suite since 2016**.

**Do not publish a years-of-experience figure.** The site contradicts itself: Karlie's own
page says *"over 20 years"*; Kaiden's page describes her as *"an industry powerhouse with
over 30 years of expertise"*. Until Karlie resolves it, the number is unusable. This is on
the [enrichment roadmap](../00-start-here/ENRICHMENT-ROADMAP.md).
