# How we work

## The four published stages

The homepage names four stages. **This is the canonical process description** — use these
four words and these definitions in any asset that explains the journey.

**1. Discovery**
> We begin with a conversation to understand your goals, financial position and property plans.

**2. Strategy**
> We assess suitable lending options and structure a finance solution aligned with your objectives.

**3. Application**
> We manage the preparation and submission of your application and guide you through the
> approval process.

**4. Settlement**
> Your loan settles, and we remain available for ongoing support and future lending reviews.

The framing that opens the section is worth keeping: *"Whether you need a first home, new
home, new car, a building loan or something to invest in for an early retirement. We've got
you covered."*

## The approach, as published

Five bullets from the homepage's "Our approach":

- Access to a broad panel of leading lenders
- Strategic loan structuring rather than simple rate comparison
- Guidance across residential, commercial and asset finance
- End-to-end support from enquiry to settlement
- Ongoing reviews to ensure your lending remains competitive

The second and fifth are the differentiated ones. The first, third and fourth are category
table stakes — every broker says them. Lead with structuring and with the post-settlement
review, not with panel size.

## The three entry points

The site offers three doors, repeated in the footer CTA block on almost every page. Any
funnel we build should map to one of them rather than inventing a fourth.

| Door | Intent | Copy used on site |
|---|---|---|
| **Check your borrowing power** | Cold, self-serve, "just exploring" | *"Just Exploring? Check your borrowing power by using our calculator via the button below."* |
| **Book a call with a broker** | Warm, wants a conversation | *"Discuss your situation and explore suitable lending options."* |
| **Start your application here** | Hot, ready to proceed | *"Provide your details securely so we can review your situation before our conversation. Start your finance fact find."* |

That three-tier ladder — **explore → talk → apply** — is the site's implicit funnel and
should be the spine of any landing page we build. See
[funnels-and-landing-pages](../08-channels-and-playbooks/funnels-and-landing-pages.md).

## The three service promises, verbatim

Repeated across the home-loan, first-home and comparison pages:

> **Competitive low rates** — We compare loans from over 60 lenders to help you secure a
> competitive rate that suits your budget and lifestyle.

> **Refinance professionals** — If your rate hasn't changed in years, you could be paying
> too much. We help you refinance with confidence and put the savings back in your pocket.

> **Complete home loan help** — From pre-approval to settlement, we handle the paperwork,
> liaise with the lender, and keep you updated throughout the entire process.

## The "Loan Health Check"

A named, repeatable offer buried on the home-loans page and under-used everywhere else:

> **Loan Health Check** — Available for when you already have a loan. Let us review it and
> show you if there's a better deal.

This is the best existing hook for the refinance audience and for reactivating a past
client. Treat it as a real product name, capitalised, not a description.

## What the process files do not tell us

The published journey stops at "we manage the application". The **actual internal
workflow** — desk allocation, packaging, serviceability, submission, MIR handling,
settlement — is documented in [11-operations/pipelines](../11-operations/pipelines.md),
which comes from Karlie's production systems rather than the website. The two describe the
same journey at different altitudes: this file is what the client sees, that file is what
the team does.

**Timeframes are not published anywhere** — not for discovery-to-submission, not for
approval, not for settlement. Do not invent them. The only timing language the site uses is
hedged: *"Some business loans can move in as little as a few hours, while more complex
applications may take longer."*
