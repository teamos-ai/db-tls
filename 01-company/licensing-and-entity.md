# Legal entity, licensing and the aggregator

**This is the single home for these facts.** Every other file links here.

> **Status is `review`, not `approved`.** Public register research on 27 Aug 2026 found
> **two active companies** behind this brand, and the one named in the published privacy
> policy is **not** the one that holds the "The Loans Suite" business name or won the 2023
> award. Every individual fact below is `verified` from a public register — **what is
> unresolved is which entity holds the credit representative authorisation.** That determines
> what belongs in the footer disclosure, so it needs Karlie's answer before any new page ships
> with a disclosure block.

## The two companies

**Three things point to this being the trading entity:** it holds the registered business name
"The Loans Suite"; it is GST-registered; and it is the name The Adviser recorded as the 2023
award winner — *"The Loans Suite Group Australia"*. The staff email domain **tlsga.com.au**
resolves to the same initials.

### Queens of Finance Pty Ltd — named in the privacy policy

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| ABN | **38 676 457 337** |
| ACN | 676 457 337 |
| Type | Australian private company |
| ABN active from | **10 April 2024** |
| GST | **Not currently registered** |
| Business name | **"The Property Suite"**, registered 5 Jun 2024 |
| Location | QLD 4020 |

The privacy policy states: *"Queens of Finance Pty Ltd T/as The Loans Suite is an Authorised
(ABN: 38676457337) Credit Representative (Credit Representative Number 477350)."*

**But Queens of Finance does not hold the "The Loans Suite" business name — it holds "The
Property Suite".** Both facts can be true at once (a credit representative authorisation can
sit with a different company from the one holding a business name), so this is not necessarily
an error. It is simply unresolved from the outside.

### Why this matters, and why it needs an answer

The published privacy policy already contains **copy-paste artifacts from another business** —
it refers to *"Domain Loan Finder"* twice and leaves the contact fields blank. Given that, the
entity name in it cannot be assumed correct without confirmation.

**Until Karlie confirms:**
- The **numbers** in the footer disclosure are safe — they are printed on every page of the
  client's own site and are unambiguous.
- **Do not name a company** in any new asset, disclosure block, contract or legal footer.
- Do not use either ABN in outward-facing material.

## The exact disclosure line

Printed in the site footer on every page. Reproduce verbatim on any page, funnel or microsite:

> Credit Representative Number 477350 is authorised under Australian Credit Licence Number 387025.

Paired with:

> Your full financial situation and requirements need to be considered prior to any offer and
> acceptance of a loan product.

**These two lines carry numbers, not company names — which is exactly why they remain safe to
use while the entity question is open.**

## What the licensing structure means for copy

The Loans Suite is a **credit representative**, not a licensee. It arranges credit; it does not
provide it, price it or approve it. Every claim boundary follows from that — see
[guardrails](../07-compliance-and-guardrails/guardrails.md) and
[nccp-and-best-interests-duty](../07-compliance-and-guardrails/nccp-and-best-interests-duty.md).

## The brand is older than both companies

Neither company predates 2023, yet:

- Karlie *"began her journey with The Loans Suite in 2016"* (her own bio)
- A **Word of Mouth review dated 27 November 2019** names The Loans Suite at a Penrith address

So the brand was trading years before either current entity existed — under an earlier company,
a sole trader, or another structure not found in these searches.

**Consequence: there is still no publishable founding year.** "Since 2016" describes Karlie's
tenure, not a company's age, and no register supports a business-age claim. Do not publish one.
