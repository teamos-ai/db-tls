repeating them.

## The aggregator — why this is `inferred`, not `verified`

A public search of ACL 387025 in August 2026 returns **Mortgage Specialists Pty Ltd**,
which trades as **Specialist Finance Group**. This is **corroborated** by the site itself:
three **Specialist Finance Group 2024 finalist badges** are displayed on the homepage
alongside the Australian Broking Awards badges. A brokerage displays its aggregator's
award badges; it does not display a competitor's.

It stays `inferred` because it has not been confirmed by the client or checked against
ASIC's Credit Licensee Register directly. **Confirm with Karlie before publishing the
aggregator's name anywhere.** For footers and disclosures, the ACL number alone is
sufficient and is `verified`.

## The exact disclosure line

This appears in the site footer on every page and must be reproduced verbatim on any page,
funnel or microsite built for the business:

> Credit Representative Number 477350 is authorised under Australian Credit Licence Number 387025.

Paired with:

> Your full financial situation and requirements need to be considered prior to any offer
> and acceptance of a loan product.

## What the licensing structure means for copy

The Loans Suite is a **credit representative**, not a licensee. It arranges credit; it does
not provide it, price it or approve it. Every claim boundary that follows from this is in
[guardrails](../07-compliance-and-guardrails/guardrails.md) and
[nccp-and-best-interests-duty](../07-compliance-and-guardrails/nccp-and-best-interests-duty.md).

## Known gaps

- **ACN not published** anywhere in the sources.
- **Date of incorporation / registration not published.** The site says Karlie *"began her
  journey with The Loans Suite in 2016"*, which dates the brand but not the entity, and
  which is not the same claim as "in business since 2016". Do not publish a founding year.
- **FBAA or MFAA membership unconfirmed.** The site embeds a Vision Abacus calculator whose
  URL path ends in `/FBAA`, which suggests an FBAA-supplied tool subscription — but a
  white-labelled calculator is not proof of membership. Do not claim either association.
