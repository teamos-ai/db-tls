## Gaps in the route map

Things the business sells or promotes that have **no page to land on**:

- **Bridging loans** — offered as a choice on the contact form, no page.
- **Refinancing** — the highest-intent commercial term in the category, with no dedicated
  page. It is a section inside `/home-loans/` and a toggle on `/home-loan-comparison/`.
  This is the single biggest addressable SEO and paid-traffic gap.
- **Loan Health Check** — a named offer with no page.
- **The two Suite hub pages** are empty shells. Anyone landing on `/solutions/the-home-suite/`
  sees nothing but CTAs.
- **No case studies, no testimonials page, no awards page.**

See [blog-and-seo](../08-channels-and-playbooks/blog-and-seo.md) and the
[enrichment roadmap](../00-start-here/ENRICHMENT-ROADMAP.md).
