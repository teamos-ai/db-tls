# Company profile

## The business in five sentences

The Loans Suite Australia is a mortgage and finance brokerage operating as an authorised
credit representative under an aggregator's Australian Credit Licence. It arranges
residential, commercial, business, asset and SMSF finance for Australian borrowers,
comparing options across a panel of **more than 60 lenders**. The service is at **no cost
to the client** — the lender pays a commission on settlement. It runs from **two offices**,
Rothwell in Queensland's Moreton Bay region and Penrith in western Sydney, with a team of
six led by Karlie Scharfenberg, and services clients Australia-wide. Its positioning is that
lending is a **structuring** problem rather than a rate-shopping problem — hence the brand
line, **"Your financial architects."**

## What it sells

Nine product lines organised into two "Suites" — the brand's own architecture:

**The Home Suite** — home loans · first home loans · investment loans ·
construction loans · SMSF lending · personal loans

**The Business Suite** — business loans · commercial finance · asset & equipment finance

Bridging loans appear as an option on the contact form but have no page of their own.
See [offer-overview](../02-offer-and-lending/offer-overview.md).

## Who it serves

Borrowers Australia-wide, with concentrations around the two offices — Moreton Bay and
North Brisbane in Queensland, and western Sydney in NSW. The published copy consistently
names **"Sydney, Brisbane and across Australia"**. Six audience segments are modelled in
[03-audience-and-icp](../03-audience-and-icp/).

## How it makes money

Lender-paid commission on settlement, plus trail. Zero cost to the client. This is stated
plainly and repeatedly on the site — *"Our service is zero-cost. We're paid by the lender
once your loan settles so you aren't left out of pocket."* Full detail and the copy rules
around it in [fee-model-and-economics](../02-offer-and-lending/fee-model-and-economics.md).

A second revenue line is **sub-broker recruitment** — brokers operating under The Loans
Suite brand on a commission split. See [sub-broker-offer](../02-offer-and-lending/sub-broker-offer.md).

## Why anyone picks them

From the published copy, four reasons, in the site's own framing:

1. **Structure over rate.** *"Lending is not just about comparing interest rates. We focus
   on structuring finance strategically to support your long-term financial goals. This is
   why we call ourselves financial architects."*
2. **Breadth of panel.** More than 60 banks, credit unions and non-bank lenders, against a
   big-four branch that offers one.
3. **They do the work.** *"Saves you time and money by avoiding endless comparison sites.
   We do the legwork."*
4. **They stay after settlement.** Ongoing reviews, loan health checks, and a stated
   intent to keep the relationship rather than close the file.
