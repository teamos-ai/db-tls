# Claims policy

Every claim in an outward-facing asset falls into one of five classes. Each has a different
evidence bar. If a claim cannot clear its bar, it does not ship.

## 1. Claims about The Loans Suite

**Bar: must trace to a `verified` file in this database.**

Publishable today: the lender panel figure ("more than 60 lenders"), the fee model
(no cost to the client, lender-paid on settlement), the two office locations, the team and
their published titles, the seven awards in their exact wording, the nine loan product
lines, the licensing details.

**Not publishable:** any number of clients, settlements, dollars written, years in business,
approval rates, or satisfaction scores. See [what-we-cannot-claim](../05-proof-and-evidence/what-we-cannot-claim.md).

## 2. Claims about a lender, product, rate or fee

**Bar: do not make them.**

The Loans Suite does not set price and does not speak for lenders. Assets may name lender
*categories* ("banks, credit unions and non-bank lenders" — the site's own phrasing) but
must not publish a rate, a fee, an LVR threshold or a policy position as fact.

Where a lender is named — as the privacy policy does with Adelaide Bank, ANZ, Bankwest and
Pepper Money — it is named as a party information may be disclosed to, not as an endorsement
or a price promise.

## 3. Claims about a client outcome

**Bar: rounded, de-identified, never framed as typical, permission on file.**

There are currently **no written case studies** and **no recorded permission status on the
five site testimonials**. Until that changes, outcome claims are limited to the exact
testimonial text already published on the site, attributed exactly as published.

Never write "clients typically save…", "most of our clients…", or a constructed
before/after. See [testimonials](../05-proof-and-evidence/testimonials.md).

## 4. Market statistics and third-party data

**Bar: source name + figure + period + URL + `researched:` date, all four, in the file.**

Every external figure lives in
[statistics-and-sources](../05-proof-and-evidence/statistics-and-sources.md) before it is
used anywhere. If a figure is not in that file, it does not go in an asset.

Re-verify before each publication. Lending statistics age in quarters, not years — the
site's own 2023 blog citing "70% market share" is now materially out of date (the figure is
81.0% for the March 2026 quarter).

## 5. Government schemes, grants and tax

**Bar: describe the scheme, never the reader's eligibility, and always point to checking.**

Scheme rules change with budgets and legislation. Copy must say what the scheme is and that
eligibility depends on rules, state and circumstances. Never "you can get $X".

Tax and superannuation content must defer to a qualified accountant, financial adviser or
SMSF specialist. See [guardrails](guardrails.md).

## The pre-ship question

For any sentence containing a number, a superlative or a promise:

> **Which file in this database does this trace to, and what tier is it?**

If the answer is "none" or "assumed", cut the sentence. Not soften it — cut it.
