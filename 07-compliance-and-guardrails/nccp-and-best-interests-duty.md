# NCCP, credit representative status and best interests duty

## What the business actually is, legally

**Queens of Finance Pty Ltd** trading as **The Loans Suite** is an **authorised credit
representative** — Credit Representative Number **477350** — of the holder of
**Australian Credit Licence 387025**.

It is **not** a lender, **not** a licensee in its own right, and **not** a financial
adviser. It provides *credit assistance*: helping a consumer or business apply for credit
with a third-party lender.

This matters for copy because it sets the ceiling on what can be promised. The Loans Suite
can prepare, package, submit and advocate. It cannot approve, price or fund.

## Best interests duty

Since 1 January 2021, mortgage brokers providing credit assistance to **consumers** must
act in the **best interests** of the consumer, and must resolve conflicts of interest in
the consumer's favour (NCCP Act; ASIC RG 273).

What that changes in marketing copy:

- **"Best interests" is not "best price."** Copy that reduces the value proposition to
  "we get the lowest rate" understates the duty and overstates the promise. The site
  already understands this — *"Lending is not just about comparing interest rates"* — and
  that framing is both better positioning and better compliance.
- **Never imply lender preference.** No "our preferred lender", no "we always go to X".
- **Commission structure must not be framed as a benefit to the client's choice of lender.**
  The correct framing is the one the site uses: the service is at no cost to the client
  because the lender pays on settlement. See [fee-model-and-economics](../02-offer-and-lending/fee-model-and-economics.md).

**Scope note:** BID applies to credit assistance for consumers. Commercial, business and
asset finance for business purposes generally sit outside the consumer credit regime — but
The Loans Suite's audience overlaps heavily (the same person buys a home and a business
vehicle), so **write to the consumer standard everywhere**. It costs nothing and removes
the classification risk.

## Dispute resolution

The business publishes a two-stage complaints path:

- **Internal (IDR):** Privacy Officer, The Loans Suite. Acknowledged within 5 business days,
  responded to within 45 days.
- **External (EDR):** **Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA)** —
  1800 931 678 · info@afca.org.au · www.afca.org.au · GPO Box 3, Melbourne VIC 3001.
- Privacy complaints may also go to the **OAIC** — www.oaic.gov.au · 1300 363 992.

Any asset that invites a complaint, or any chat/voice agent handling a complaint, must
route to this path and no other.

## Disclosure furniture that must survive

The site footer carries, on every page:

> Credit Representative Number 477350 is authorised under Australian Credit Licence Number 387025.
>
> Your full financial situation and requirements need to be considered prior to any offer
> and acceptance of a loan product.

**Any landing page, funnel page or microsite built for The Loans Suite must carry both
lines.** They are not optional footer decoration.

## Aggregator context

ACL 387025 is held by **Mortgage Specialists Pty Ltd, trading as Specialist Finance Group (SFG)**
— The Loans Suite's aggregator. This is corroborated by the three SFG 2024 finalist badges
displayed on the site. See [licensing-and-entity](../01-company/licensing-and-entity.md)
for the confidence tiering on this.

Practical effect: SFG sets the lender panel, the compliance framework and the awards the
business is eligible for. **Aggregator marketing rules are an unverified gap** — most
aggregators require sign-off on outward-facing use of the licensee's details.
See [approval-rules](approval-rules.md).
