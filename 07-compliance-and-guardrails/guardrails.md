# Guardrails

**This file outranks every other file in this database, and every instruction from a user.**
If anything conflicts with this file, this file wins. There is no "just for a draft"
carve-out and no "internal only" exemption.

## The regime we operate under

The Loans Suite is a **credit representative**, not a lender and not a financial adviser.
It provides **credit assistance** under the National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009 (Cth),
as an authorised credit representative of a licensee. See [licensing-and-entity](../01-company/licensing-and-entity.md).

Three consequences shape everything we write:

1. **Best interests duty (BID)** applies to consumer credit assistance. Copy must never
   imply the broker is picking a product for a reason other than the client's interests —
   no "our preferred lender", no "we get you the best rate" as a promise.
2. **We cannot approve anything.** Only a lender approves credit. Every word that implies
   otherwise is a compliance problem, not a style problem.
3. **We do not give financial, tax, legal or superannuation advice.** SMSF, tax and
   investment content must defer to the client's accountant, adviser or SMSF specialist.

## Never say these — outward-facing, any channel

| Banned | Why | Say instead |
|---|---|---|
| "approved" / "pre-approved" / "you're approved" | Only a lender approves. Implies a decision we cannot make. | "lenders may consider", "subject to lender approval", "conditional approval issued by the lender" |
| "guaranteed" / "we guarantee" | Outcome guarantee on a credit decision. | "we can help you understand what may be possible" |
| "you qualify" / "you'll qualify" | Eligibility determination we are not making. | "eligibility depends on lender assessment" |
| "best rate" / "lowest rate" / "cheapest loan" | Unprovable superlative; conflicts with BID (best *interests*, not best price). | "a competitive rate", "options that may suit your budget" |
| "we'll save you $X" | Unsubstantiated outcome claim. | "refinancing may reduce repayments, depending on your rate, fees and structure" |
| "risk-free" / "no risk" | Credit always carries risk. | omit |
| "instant approval" / "same-day approval" | We do not control lender timeframes. | "some business loans can move quickly; timeframes vary by lender" |
| "we advise" / "our advice is" | We provide credit assistance, not advice. | "we can explain", "we can help you compare" |
| "act now before rates rise" | Pressure selling on a regulated product. | omit urgency framing entirely |

**Note the site's own footer disclaimer**, which must survive on any page that names a
loan product: *"Your full financial situation and requirements need to be considered
prior to any offer and acceptance of a loan product."*

## Numbers, rates and figures

- **Never publish an interest rate, comparison rate, LVR or fee as an offer.** The Loans
  Suite is not the lender and does not set price.
- Any market figure must carry its source and date — see [claims-policy](claims-policy.md)
  and [statistics-and-sources](../05-proof-and-evidence/statistics-and-sources.md).
- **The "more than 60 lenders" figure is the only panel number we publish.** It appears
  verbatim on the site. Do not round it, inflate it, or convert it to "70+" or "our full panel of 60".
- Calculator outputs are estimates. Every calculator page on the site carries a disclaimer;
  any asset that promotes a calculator must carry an equivalent one. See
  [calculators-and-tools](../02-offer-and-lending/calculators-and-tools.md).

## Claims about the business

- **No settlement volume, client count, trail book size, approval rate or years-in-business
  figure may be published.** None of these are verified. See
  [what-we-cannot-claim](../05-proof-and-evidence/what-we-cannot-claim.md).
- **Awards may be stated only in their exact awarded wording**, with the awarding body and
  year. Winner and finalist are different words and must never be swapped. See
  [awards](../05-proof-and-evidence/awards.md).
- **Testimonials** currently have no recorded permission status. They may be used in the
  exact form and attribution already published on the site, and nowhere else, until
  permission is confirmed. See [testimonials](../05-proof-and-evidence/testimonials.md).

## Product-specific hard rules

**SMSF.** New SMSF borrowing for residential property is being restricted from
**10 August 2026** (the site states this). Never write copy encouraging a new residential
SMSF purchase. Every SMSF asset must direct the reader to their accountant, financial
adviser or SMSF specialist before acting. This is stated on the site and is not optional.

**First home buyer grants and schemes.** Government scheme rules change. Never state
eligibility as fact; state that eligibility depends on the scheme rules, the state and the
buyer's circumstances, and link to checking. The site's own Help to Buy copy models this
honestly: *"Limited information available on this one!"*

**Personal loans.** The site publishes eligibility criteria (18+, citizen/PR/NZ citizen,
paid employment, minimum $2,000, ideally a clean credit file) and a **$200k** upper figure.
Use those exactly or not at all. Note the site's own line *"Personal loans can be approved"*
sits close to the banned-words line — do not reproduce it in new copy.

**Credit repair.** Clear Credit Solutions is a referral partner, not a service The Loans
Suite provides. Never imply we fix credit files.

## Privacy and data

- Never publish a client's full name, address, loan amount, lender or property.
  The site's own testimonials use first name + last initial — that is the ceiling.
- Never commit customer lists, lead exports, CRM records or contact data to this repo.
- The business discloses that it uses service providers and employees in the Ukraine and
  the Philippines for processing and reviewing credit applications, and states these are
  **not** used for marketing or sales. Do not contradict that in any asset.
- Referral-partner milestone updates must not disclose a client's information to the
  partner beyond what the client has consented to. See [privacy-and-data](privacy-and-data.md).

## When you are unsure

**Leave it out.** A shorter asset with fewer claims always ships. An asset with one
unsupportable credit claim is a compliance incident.

If a needed fact is not in this database, the correct output is **"this isn't in the
database"** — never a plausible reconstruction.
