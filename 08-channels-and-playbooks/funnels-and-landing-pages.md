# Playbook — funnels and landing pages

## Purpose

Build a standalone landing page or funnel to convert paid or referred traffic into a booked
call or a captured lead.

## The rule that governs everything

**Respect the commitment ladder: explore → talk → apply.**

The site's own three CTAs map to it exactly. A funnel that asks a cold visitor to "start your
application" skips two rungs and will not convert. Match the ask to the temperature:

| Traffic temperature | The ask |
|---|---|
| Cold (paid social, display) | **Explore** — a calculator, a number, no name required until the result |
| Warm (search, referral, retargeting) | **Talk** — book a 15-minute call |
| Hot (partner referral, existing client) | **Apply** — the fact find |

## Formats

| Format | Length | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Calculator capture page | ~200 words + tool | Cold. Result gated behind a short form |
| Booking landing page | 400–600 words | Warm. One offer, one CTA repeated |
| Long-form landing page | 900–1,400 words | Cold-to-warm on a complex offer (SMSF, commercial, recruitment) |
| Partner co-brand page | 300–500 words | Referred traffic. Both logos, partner named |

## The universal structure

1. **Headline** — the outcome the reader wants, in their words, not ours. Under 12 words.
2. **Sub** — one sentence on what happens and what it costs (nothing).
3. **CTA #1** — above the fold.
4. **Three reasons** — 25–40 words each.
5. **The micro-commitment step** — the calculator, the three questions, the "which of these
   are you?" fork. **This is what separates a funnel from a page.**
6. **Proof** — one testimonial, or the 2023 award. One, not a wall.
7. **What happens next** — remove uncertainty. Three steps, one line each.
8. **Objection block** — 2–3 short Q&As from [objections](../10-faq-and-objections/objections.md).
9. **CTA #2** — same offer, same words as CTA #1.
10. **Compliance footer** — both disclosure lines.

## The micro-commitment pattern

Three low-stakes questions before asking for contact details. Proven in the business's own
best asset, the equity calculator, whose intent question is genuinely good segmentation:

> How would you like to use your equity? Buy investment · Refinance · Debt consolidation ·
> Renovate · **Not sure, just checking what I have**

That last option is why it works — it gives permission to be uncommitted, which is where most
traffic actually is.

## Do

- One offer per page. One.
- Repeat the same CTA words at top and bottom
- Name what happens after they submit — *"one of our brokers will call you"*
- Use the "not sure, just checking" escape hatch on every segmentation question
- Route the booking to the right broker by product and state
- Carry the calculator disclaimer wherever a number is produced

## Don't

- Don't ask for a phone number before showing value
- Don't put a rate, a saving or an approval promise anywhere
- Don't stack multiple offers
- Don't use a countdown timer or false scarcity on a credit product
- Don't send cold traffic to the generic contact form — it converts to nothing
- Don't produce a "savings report". A **comparison** or **review**, never a saving

## Pre-send checklist

- [ ] One offer, one CTA, repeated verbatim
- [ ] The ask matches the traffic temperature
- [ ] A micro-commitment step exists before the contact form
- [ ] Zero banned words; no rate, saving or approval language
- [ ] Booking routes to the correct broker
- [ ] Both compliance footer lines present
- [ ] Calculator disclaimer present if any figure is shown
- [ ] **Hardship path:** if the funnel can surface financial distress, the National Debt
      Helpline (1800 007 007) is offered alongside the call
- [ ] Mobile checked — most traffic is
- [ ] Testimonial used is one of the six, verbatim, attributed as published

## Worked example — the Equity Check funnel

**Traffic:** Meta, cold, homeowners 35–60 in Moreton Bay and western Sydney.
**Pillar 3 → 2.** ICP: [icp-property-investor](../03-audience-and-icp/icp-property-investor.md)
and [icp-refinancer](../03-audience-and-icp/icp-refinancer.md).

---

**H1:** You might have more equity than you think.

**Sub:** Find out roughly how much of your home you actually own, and what you could do with
it. Takes about a minute, costs nothing.

**CTA:** `Check my equity`

**Step 1 (no form):** *What's your property worth, roughly?* → slider
**Step 2:** *What's still owing on your loan?* → slider
**Step 3:** *What would you use it for?* → Buy an investment · Renovate · Refinance ·
Consolidate debts · **Not sure, just checking what I have**

**Result screen:** "Based on what you've entered, your estimated equity is approximately
$X." + *"This is an estimate only. Usable equity depends on your income, expenses, loan
balance and lender criteria."*

**Then:** *Want to know how much of that is actually usable? A broker can tell you in a
15-minute call. It costs nothing.* → name, email, mobile → `Book my call`

**Three reasons**
*Not all equity is usable* — Lenders decide how much you can access based on your income,
expenses and their own criteria. We'll tell you the realistic number.
*More than 60 lenders* — Different lenders take very different views. Your current one is
just one opinion.
*It costs you nothing* — We're paid by the lender if and when a loan settles. Not by you.

**Proof:** *"We have had the most amazing experience working with the team. All of our
questions have been answered in a fast and easy to comprehend way."* — Senna F

**What happens next:** 1. We call you at a time that suits. 2. We review your position and
your options. 3. If it's worth doing something, we handle it. If it isn't, we say so.

**CTA:** `Check my equity`

**Footer:** both disclosure lines.
