# Playbook — website and page copy

## Purpose

Produce a page for theloanssuite.com.au — a product page, a location page, or a new page for
an offer that currently has none.

## Formats and lengths

| Page type | Words | Sections |
|---|---|---|
| Product / solution page | 700–1,100 | Hero · 3 benefits · "What you need to know" · FAQ · closing CTA |
| Location page | 600–900 | Hero (suburb named) · why local matters · 3 benefits · FAQ · CTA |
| Calculator page | 500–800 above the tool, 400–700 below | Tool · disclaimer · explainer · FAQ |
| Hub page | 300–500 | Intro · card grid · CTA |
| Landing page | See [funnels-and-landing-pages](funnels-and-landing-pages.md) | |

## The universal structure

The site's best pages — home loans, first home, construction — all follow the same shape.
**Use it.**

**1. Hero.** One sentence naming the reader's situation, one sentence on what we do about it,
two CTAs (a soft one and a hard one).
> Buying your first home is exciting, but it can also come with a lot of questions around
> deposits, grants, borrowing capacity and loan options. The Loans Suite helps first home
> buyers understand what may be possible and move through the process with clear guidance
> from start to settlement.

**2. Three benefit blocks.** Heading of 2–5 words, then 25–40 words. Always three.

**3. "What you need to know about X."** Three hedged bullets naming what actually affects the
outcome. This is where honesty about complexity does its work.

**4. Body sections.** Two or three H2s of 100–200 words each. This is the SEO body. Name
Sydney and Brisbane here.

**5. FAQ.** Three to six questions, drawn from [faq](../10-faq-and-objections/faq.md).
Paraphrase for phrasing, **never change substance or hedging**.

**6. Closing CTA.** A question as the heading, two sentences, one button.
> Planning to build or renovate? The Loans Suite can help you understand your construction
> loan options, prepare for lender requirements and move through each stage with clear guidance.

## Do

- Open on the reader's situation, never on the business
- Use "you" and "we"
- Hedge with "may", "can", "depending on"
- Name **Sydney, Brisbane and across Australia** on any page with local intent
- Link to the relevant calculator
- Use the site's own conversational CTA style — *"Let's talk commercial"*, *"Help me build my portfolio"*
- Keep paragraphs to 2–4 sentences

## Don't

- Don't state a rate, fee or LVR
- Don't write "approved", "guaranteed", "qualify", "best rate"
- Don't use *tailored, bespoke, solutions, seamless, journey, unlock, boutique*
- Don't claim a business metric — see [what-we-cannot-claim](../05-proof-and-evidence/what-we-cannot-claim.md)
- Don't stack more than two CTAs in a section
- Don't use American spelling

## Pre-send checklist

- [ ] Maps to exactly one messaging pillar
- [ ] Zero banned words (search the draft for the list)
- [ ] Every factual claim traces to a `verified` file — no `assumed` content present
- [ ] No rate, fee, LVR or approval language
- [ ] Australian English throughout
- [ ] Panel figure written as "more than 60 lenders" if used
- [ ] Footer disclosure present: credit rep line **and** the "full financial situation" line
- [ ] If a calculator is referenced, its disclaimer or a link to it is present
- [ ] FAQ substance matches [faq](../10-faq-and-objections/faq.md)
- [ ] Internal links resolve against [site-map-and-routes](../01-company/site-map-and-routes.md)
- [ ] Word count within range

## Worked example — the refinance page that does not exist

**Brief:** `/solutions/the-home-suite/refinance/`. Pillar 2 — we do the work.
ICP: [icp-refinancer](../03-audience-and-icp/icp-refinancer.md).

---

**H1:** Refinancing, without the paperwork landing on you.

**Sub:** If your rate hasn't changed in years, you could be paying too much. The Loans Suite
reviews your current loan against options from more than 60 lenders, and handles the
paperwork, the lender and the follow-up if it's worth moving.

**CTAs:** `Book a Loan Health Check` · `Check your equity`

**Three benefits**

*Find out where you stand* — A Loan Health Check reviews your existing loan against current
options. If moving isn't worth it, we'll tell you that too.

*We handle the process* — From application to settlement, we prepare the paperwork, liaise
with the lender and keep you updated. You don't chase anyone.

*More than 60 lenders compared* — Banks, credit unions and non-bank lenders, so you're not
limited to what your current lender is willing to offer you.

**What you need to know about refinancing**

- Switching can involve exit costs, application fees and government charges, which may offset
  a lower rate
- Lenders reassess your income, expenses and debts, so your position may have changed since
  you first borrowed
- A lower rate is not always the better outcome if you lose features you rely on

**Body H2:** *Why loans drift out of date* — front-book and back-book pricing, life changes,
the fixed-rate cliff for anyone who borrowed 2020–2022. 150 words.

**Body H2:** *Refinancing in Sydney, Brisbane and across Australia* — 150 words.

**FAQ:** Will refinancing hurt my credit score? · How long does it take? · What does it cost
me to use a broker? · Should I just ask my current lender for a better rate?

**Close:** *Not sure if your loan is still competitive?*
The Loans Suite can review it and tell you plainly whether moving is worth it. It costs you
nothing — we're paid by the lender if and when your loan settles.
`Book a Loan Health Check`
