# Playbook — organic social

## Purpose

Produce an organic social post — static, carousel or short-form video.

## The live channels

- **Instagram: @the.loans.suite**
- **Facebook: facebook.com/theloanssuitesydney**

**No LinkedIn, TikTok or YouTube presence exists in the sources.** Do not write for a channel
that does not exist. **LinkedIn is the notable gap** — it is where referral partners and
sub-broker recruits actually are. See
[icp-sub-broker-recruit](../03-audience-and-icp/icp-sub-broker-recruit.md).

## Formats

| Format | Spec |
|---|---|
| Static | 1080×1080 or 1080×1350. Caption 40–120 words |
| Carousel | 4–8 slides. Slide 1 is the hook. 8–20 words per slide |
| Reel / short video | 20–45 seconds. Hook in the first 2 seconds. Captions burned in |
| Story | 1080×1920. One idea. A poll or question sticker doubles engagement |

## Design constraints

Dark canvas `#1A1411`. Champagne `#D1BCA0` accents. Cream `#F2ECE4` for inverted slides.
Canela Thin for the headline, large and weight 100. Stack Sans Text for body.
**Zero border radius. No drop shadows.**

## Content pillars

**1. The reality check** (video, Karlie to camera). The most differentiated format available —
a named, awarded broker saying something concrete that a bank won't.
- *"Why your bank won't lower your rate until you threaten to leave."*
- *"Why a $50k pay rise doesn't increase your borrowing power as much as you'd think."*
- *"The mistake first home buyers in Queensland make with government grants."*

**2. Behind the deal** (carousel). Challenge → obstacle → what got solved → outcome.
**De-identified, structural, no figures, never framed as typical.**

**3. Explain one term** (static or 15-second video). LMI, offset, comparison rate,
pre-approval versus approval, usable equity. Highest-saving, highest-sharing format.

**4. The team** (photo, story). Real people. The library of professional portraits already
exists. Kaiden's generational story is genuinely distinctive.

**5. Local market** (static). Moreton Bay and western Sydney. **Sourced figures only.**

**6. Proof** (static). Awards, and testimonials in the exact published form.

## Do

- Hook in the first line, before the fold
- One idea per post
- Speak to one person
- Use the client's vocabulary — *fast, simple, sorted, explained*
- Caption every video. Most viewing is silent
- Post the human stuff. It outperforms the educational stuff and builds the referral base
- Reply to every comment

## Don't

- Don't post a rate, a saving, or an approval claim
- Don't post a client's details, property, or loan amount — even with permission, keep it de-identified
- Don't use trending audio on a compliance-sensitive topic
- Don't do fear-based hooks. See [buyer-psychology](../03-audience-and-icp/buyer-psychology.md)
- Don't name or subtweet a competitor or a lender
- Don't use stock-broker clichés — handshakes, tiny houses on calculators, arrows going up
- Don't stack hashtags. Five to eight, relevant

## Pre-send checklist

- [ ] One idea, one hook, one CTA
- [ ] Zero banned words; no rate, saving or approval language
- [ ] Any figure appears in [statistics-and-sources](../05-proof-and-evidence/statistics-and-sources.md), sourced on-slide
- [ ] Any client reference de-identified, no figures, not framed as typical
- [ ] Brand colours and fonts; zero radius
- [ ] Video captioned
- [ ] Testimonials verbatim and attributed as published
- [ ] Awards in exact wording with year and body
- [ ] Australian English

## Worked example — Reel

**Pillar 1. ICP: refinancer. Pillar 2 (we do the work). 30 seconds.**

---

**Hook (0–2s, on screen and spoken):**
> Your bank has one answer. We've got sixty.

**Body (2–25s, Karlie to camera):**
> Here's the thing nobody tells you. When your bank says you can't borrow that much — that's
> not a fact about you. That's one lender's assessment rules.
>
> Every lender treats income differently. Overtime, commission, casual work, self-employed
> income — some count it, some shade it, some won't touch it.
>
> So a no from one lender genuinely isn't a no. It's one opinion out of sixty.

**Close (25–30s):**
> If you've been told no, it's worth a second look. Costs you nothing to find out.

**Caption:**
> A no from your bank is one lender's opinion, not a verdict.
>
> We compare more than 60 banks, credit unions and non-bank lenders — and they assess income
> very differently from each other. Especially if yours isn't a straightforward salary.
>
> Worth a conversation. It costs you nothing — we're paid by the lender if a loan settles.
>
> #mortgagebroker #homeloans #firsthomebuyer #brisbane #penrith #refinance
