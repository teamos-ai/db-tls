# Playbook — blog and SEO

## Purpose

Write a blog article or an SEO landing page for theloanssuite.com.au.

## What already exists

**171 published posts** across six categories: `finance-101` · `home-loans` ·
`business-finance` · `investing` · `saving-tips` · `smsfs`.

The existing content is competent, hedged and compliant — genuinely a good base. Its two
weaknesses are that some of it is **dated** (a 2023 post still headlines "70% of home buyers
turned to a Mortgage Broker", now 81%) and that **almost none of it targets local intent**.

## Formats

| Type | Words | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Explainer | 800–1,200 | Answer one question thoroughly. Rank for it |
| News / change | 500–800 | A rule or rate change, dated. Timely |
| Guide | 1,200–1,800 | A whole process end to end. Link hub |
| Local page | 600–900 | Suburb or region intent |
| Comparison | 900–1,400 | "X vs Y" — high commercial intent |

## Universal structure

1. **H1** — the question or the claim, as the reader would phrase it
2. **Opening (60–100 words)** — the answer up front. Do not bury it
3. **H2 sections** — one idea each, 150–250 words
4. **Bulleted "what to consider"** — hedged, scannable
5. **A local paragraph** — Sydney, Brisbane and across Australia
6. **FAQ** — 3–6 questions matching real search phrasing
7. **Close** — one CTA to the relevant calculator or a call

## The three biggest SEO gaps

**1. Local pages — the clearest miss.** The closest competitor runs suburb-level landing pages
for Redcliffe, Clontarf, Kippa-Ring and Margate. The Loans Suite has **none**, despite two
physical offices.

Build: `mortgage broker Rothwell` · `Redcliffe` · `North Lakes` · `Kippa-Ring` · `Newport` ·
`Moreton Bay` · `North Brisbane` — and `Penrith` · `western Sydney`.
Two of the referral partners (McGrath North Lakes, Belle Property Newport) corroborate the
QLD footprint.

**2. No refinance page.** The highest commercial-intent term in the category has no dedicated
page — it lives as a section inside `/home-loans/`. See the worked example in
[website-and-page-copy](website-and-page-copy.md).

**3. Business finance is under-weighted.** Commercial and asset finance lead the homepage's own
solutions ordering, and the blog is dominated by residential. The
**"full-stack finance adviser"** angle from the business's own SME post is unexploited.

## Do

- Answer the question in the first paragraph. Then explain
- Use the site's existing definitions from [glossary](../06-competitors-and-market/glossary.md)
- Date anything time-sensitive, in the copy, not just the metadata
- Link to the relevant calculator and product page
- Name real locations for local intent
- Write FAQ questions the way people actually type them
- Keep the hedging. It is both compliance and honesty

## Don't

- Don't publish an unsourced statistic
- Don't publish a rate, or a rate forecast
- Don't give tax, investment or superannuation advice — refer to an accountant or adviser
- Don't write "in today's market" — date it or cut it
- Don't republish an old post without re-verifying its figures
- Don't keyword-stuff a suburb name. Three or four natural uses

## Pre-publish checklist

- [ ] Every figure appears in [statistics-and-sources](../05-proof-and-evidence/statistics-and-sources.md), cited with source and period in the copy
- [ ] No rate, forecast, approval or saving claim
- [ ] Zero banned words
- [ ] Tax, super and investment topics defer to a qualified professional
- [ ] Time-sensitive content carries a visible date
- [ ] Links to the relevant calculator and product page
- [ ] Meta title under 60 characters, description under 155
- [ ] Australian English
- [ ] One CTA at the close
- [ ] Both compliance footer lines present on the template

## The maintenance job nobody does

**Re-verify before republishing.** Three live posts carry figures that have moved:

- *"70% of home buyers turned to a Mortgage Broker last quarter"* (Aug 2023) — the figure is
  now **81.0%** (March 2026 quarter). Update the headline or date the post prominently
- The SMSF pages reference a restriction "from 10 August 2026" as **forthcoming**. That date
  has passed
- The stamp duty calculator runs on rates as at **1 July 2025**

**A dated statistic presented as current is the most common way a compliant business publishes
something misleading.** Set a quarterly review.

## Worked example — brief for a local page

**Target:** `mortgage broker Redcliffe` · **Route:** `/mortgage-broker-redcliffe/` ·
**Words:** ~750

- **H1:** Mortgage brokers in Redcliffe and the Moreton Bay region
- **Open (80 words):** We're based in Rothwell, minutes from Redcliffe. Name the two things a
  local broker actually knows: the local market and which lenders are comfortable with it
- **H2 — What we help with locally:** first home buyers, upgraders, investors, construction,
  business owners. Link each to its product page
- **H2 — Buying in Redcliffe and Moreton Bay:** median price context, **sourced and dated**.
  Deposit implications. Link to the stamp duty and borrowing power calculators
- **H2 — More than 60 lenders, from an office up the road:** the panel argument, locally framed
- **H2 — Working with local partners:** McGrath North Lakes and Belle Property Newport, both
  already published on `/partners/`
- **FAQ:** Do I need to come into the office? · What does it cost? · Do you only do home
  loans? · Do you work with buyers outside Redcliffe?
- **Close:** office address, phone, book a call

**Checklist note:** the median-price figure is currently `inferred` from secondary reporting.
**Verify against CoreLogic/Cotality or realestate.com.au before publishing**, or cut it.
