# Competitors

> **This folder shapes *how we say things*, never *who we attack*.** Naming a competitor in
> anything outward-facing is banned. See
> [banned-language](../04-voice-and-messaging/banned-language.md).

## The three competitor types that actually matter

The Loans Suite does not primarily lose to another local brokerage. It loses to a
**category of alternative**. Three of them:

**1. The bank branch / direct channel.** Still 19% of the market. Wins on incumbency and
inertia. Structurally cannot compare — one lender, one answer.

**2. Digital brokers and comparison portals.** Lendi, comparison sites, bank aggregator tools.
Win on speed and zero friction. Structurally cannot advocate — nobody works overnight to save
your settlement.

**3. Franchise and network brokerages.** Aussie, Loan Market, Mortgage Choice, and local
independents like Borro. Compete on the same axis. Differentiated by panel size, commercial
capability and independence.

**Where The Loans Suite genuinely leads: product range and independence.** It is the only
column covering residential, commercial, business, asset and SMSF under one principal — and the
only one holding a national **Independent** Office of the Year win, a category the two franchise
columns are structurally ineligible for.

**Where it genuinely trails — three things, corrected 2026-08-27:**

1. **Panel size, locally.** Loan Market Aqua claims 100+ against our 60+, on the same Moreton
   Bay footprint, and leads with it. **Panel size is no longer a claim to lead on.**
2. **Published social proof.** Borro publishes 215+ Google reviews; The Loans Suite publishes
   six testimonials.
3. **Speed to a first number.** Borro offers a "Free Loan Assessment in 30 Minutes". We make no
   equivalent promise.

## The two claims competitors make that are worth answering

**"30-minute free assessment" (Borro).** A speed promise The Loans Suite does not make. The
answer is not to match it but to reframe: a fast number is easy; a right structure takes a
conversation. **Do not invent a competing time promise** —
[guardrails](../07-compliance-and-guardrails/guardrails.md).

**"We do all the hard work" (Aussie, Borro, and essentially everyone).** This is Pillar 2,
and it is not differentiated. The Loans Suite's version has to be more specific to land:
*"we handle the paperwork, liaise with the lender, and keep you updated"* is better than
"we do the hard work" because it names three things.

## How to use these teardowns

1. **To avoid their vocabulary.** Everything the category says is in
   [banned-language](../04-voice-and-messaging/banned-language.md). If three competitors use
   a phrase, we cannot.
2. **To find the unoccupied position.** Nobody uses "financial architects". Nobody leads with
   commercial-plus-residential under one principal. Nobody says "it can also be lonely" to
   brokers.
3. **To calibrate proof.** Borro's 215+ reviews set the benchmark our five testimonials must
   eventually meet.
