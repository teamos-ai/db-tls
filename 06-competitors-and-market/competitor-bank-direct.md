# The bank direct channel

**Type:** Going straight to a lender — branch, call centre, app or mobile lender.

**Size:** **19% of new residential home lending** in the March 2026 quarter, the residual
after brokers' 81.0% share. See
[statistics-and-sources](../05-proof-and-evidence/statistics-and-sources.md).

Shrinking fast — broker share has risen 25.7 percentage points since 2018 — but 19% of a
very large market is still the largest single alternative to using a broker at all.

## Why people still go direct

- **Existing relationship.** Their salary lands there. It feels like the default.
- **Perceived loyalty benefit** — a belief that being a long-standing customer earns a better
  rate. It generally does the opposite.
- **Perceived simplicity.** One conversation, one entity, no middle party.
- **Distrust of brokers**, usually rooted in not understanding who pays.

## The structural weakness — the whole argument in one sentence

**A bank can only offer you the bank's product.**

There is no counter to this. A branch lender who cannot service your loan cannot send you
anywhere. A broker with 60+ lenders can. Aussie's version of this line —
*"Your current bank isn't your only option"* — is the cleanest phrasing in the market, and
The Loans Suite needs its own.

The second weakness: **front-book versus back-book pricing.** New customers routinely get
sharper rates than existing ones at the same institution. This is well documented and widely
reported, and it is the reason "ask your bank for a better rate" is genuinely good advice
that most people never act on.

**Compliance note:** describe the mechanism, never accuse a named bank. Never write *"the big
four are ripping you off"* — see [banned-language](../04-voice-and-messaging/banned-language.md).

## The three angles that work

1. **"Your bank has one answer. We have sixty."** Simple, true, unanswerable.
2. **"Your bank isn't going to tell you when its own rate stops being competitive."**
   Which is exactly what the Loan Health Check and the retention pipeline exist to do.
3. **"It costs you nothing to check."** Pillar 3. The person going direct assumes a broker
   costs money. See [fee-model-and-economics](../02-offer-and-lending/fee-model-and-economics.md).

## The one thing never to do

**Never disparage a lender by name.** Every lender on the panel is a business partner, the
aggregator relationship depends on lender goodwill, and it reads as unprofessional.
Compare structurally, never personally.
