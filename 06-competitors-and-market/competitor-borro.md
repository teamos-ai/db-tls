# Borro

**Type:** Independent brokerage, North Brisbane. **The closest direct competitor** — they
run a dedicated Redcliffe landing page targeting the exact suburb footprint of The Loans
Suite's Rothwell office.

## What they publish, verbatim

> **Local Experts Guiding You Home**

> Borro has access to 30+ lenders, ensuring that you get the best deal available.

> No hidden fees, no jargon. We make the home loan process clear and easy to understand.

> **Get a Free Loan Assessment in 30 Minutes**

> We do all the hard work, so you can make an informed decision.

Social proof: **215+ Google Reviews**, 5-star.

## What they do well

- **Suburb-level landing pages.** A page for Redcliffe, and for Clontarf, Kippa-Ring,
  Margate and surrounds. This is a programmatic local SEO play and it is the single
  clearest thing The Loans Suite is not doing.
- **215+ Google reviews.** Against five published testimonials, this is a proof gap of two
  orders of magnitude — and it is the gap most visible to a consumer comparing on Google.
- **A concrete, time-bounded offer.** "Free Loan Assessment in 30 Minutes" gives a cold
  visitor a specific, low-commitment thing to say yes to. The Loans Suite's equivalent
  ("Book a call with a broker") is vaguer.
- **"No jargon"** is a genuinely good promise for this audience.

## Their weaknesses

- **30+ lenders against 60+.** We win the panel comparison.
- **Residential-only.** No commercial, business, asset or SMSF offer.
- **"Ensuring that you get the best deal available"** is a superlative outcome promise that
  sits awkwardly against best interests duty. **We must not match it** —
  see [guardrails](../07-compliance-and-guardrails/guardrails.md).
- No published award recognition of the weight of a national Independent Office of the Year win.

## What we take from this — three concrete actions

1. **Suburb pages are the local SEO gap.** Borro has them; The Loans Suite has none. Rothwell,
   Redcliffe, Kippa-Ring, North Lakes, Newport, Margate, Clontarf — plus Penrith and western
   Sydney. See [blog-and-seo](../08-channels-and-playbooks/blog-and-seo.md).
2. **The review gap is the most urgent proof problem in the business.** Five testimonials
   versus 215+ reviews is decisive for any consumer who checks. A review-generation process
   should run from Michelle's post-settlement workflow.
3. **A named, time-bounded free offer converts better than "book a call."** The **Loan Health
   Check** is already a named offer with no page and no promise attached. Giving it a shape
   would answer Borro's 30-minute assessment without matching their outcome language.
