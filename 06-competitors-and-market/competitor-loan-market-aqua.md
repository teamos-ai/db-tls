# Loan Market Aqua

> **Tier note:** `inferred`, and thinner than the other teardowns. The competitor's site
> blocked automated access. What follows comes from search summaries and directory
> listings. **Re-do this teardown manually before relying on it.**

**Type:** Loan Market franchise operating across the Moreton Bay region — Redcliffe, Margate
and surrounds. Same geographic footprint as the Rothwell office.

## What is known

- Lead broker **Ronnie Singh**, described in listings as having over a decade in finance.
- Strong five-star review presence, with reviews emphasising thoroughness and clear
  communication.
- Loan Market is a major national franchise network (part of the Loan Market Group), so
  panel and compliance are set at network level.
- Franchise branding with a local broker's name attached — the standard network model.

## Where The Loans Suite differs

- **Independent, not franchised.** The Adviser's 2023 **Independent Office of the Year**
  win is precisely the recognition a franchise cannot hold. This is the sharpest available
  contrast and it never needs to name anyone.
- **Product range.** Franchise residential brokers rarely carry commercial, asset and SMSF.
- **Panel.** 60+ is likely larger than the network panel, but **this is unverified** and must
  not be claimed.

## The lesson, not the attack

The local franchise competitor competes on **the individual broker's reputation plus a
national brand's trust**. The Loans Suite has the individual broker (Karlie, with a national
award) but is trading a national brand for independence.

**"Independent" needs to be positioned as a benefit, not a fact.** Independence means the
panel is not set by a franchisor's commercial arrangements and the person you meet owns the
outcome. That argument is available and unused.
