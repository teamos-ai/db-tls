# Loan Market Aqua

**Type:** Loan Market franchise serving Woody Point, Redcliffe, Margate and the Moreton Bay
region — the same footprint as The Loans Suite's Rothwell office.

*(The first build of this file was `inferred` because the site blocked automated access. It has
now been read directly and is `verified`.)*

## The finding that changes the competitive picture

> **Access over 100 lenders in one place.**

> With **100+ banks and lenders** on our panel, we can find competitive solutions for most
> scenarios.

> Access to **over 90+ banks and lenders** in one place

Their own page is internally inconsistent — 100, 100+, 90+ — but every version is **larger than
The Loans Suite's "more than 60."**

**This overturns an assumption in the first build.** Panel breadth reads as a differentiator
against Aussie (25+) and Borro (30+), and it is. **It is not a differentiator against the local
Loan Market franchise, which claims roughly double.** Any local campaign leading on panel size
walks straight into a bigger number.

Corrections have been made to [lender-panel](../02-offer-and-lending/lender-panel.md) and
[positioning](../04-voice-and-messaging/positioning.md).

## The team

Five people — comparable in size to The Loans Suite:

**Ronnie Singh** (Owner/Broker, over a decade in finance) · **Hayley Faull** ·
**Matthew Wade** (Credit Analyst) · **Liza Quimpo** (Broker Support Officer) ·
**Racheal Kunde** (Client Service Manager)

Note the **dedicated Credit Analyst** — a specialist role The Loans Suite does not publish.

## What they publish

> We don't work for shareholders or a bank – we work in your best interest.

> We've been helping Aussies and Kiwis achieve their ambitions since **1994**.

> From understanding your goals to settlement and beyond… **Understand your goals · Provide you
> with some options · Get it sorted for you · Help you save**

They run a **side-by-side comparison table** — "Loan Market vs Banks & lenders" — ticking
non-bank-affiliation, panel access and post-settlement support.

Their form asks **"How did you find us?"** with options including Google, customer review site,
social media, referral, local event. That is attribution capture The Loans Suite's contact form
does not do.

## What they do well

- **Panel size, and they lead with it.** It is the first line on the page.
- **A four-step process that ends on "Help you save"** — an ongoing-review promise, similar to
  the Loan Health Check but actually stated on the page.
- **Named team with roles and direct numbers**, same as The Loans Suite.
- **A 1994 heritage claim.** Franchise-level, but it is a date, and The Loans Suite cannot
  currently publish any equivalent — see
  [licensing-and-entity](../01-company/licensing-and-entity.md).
- **Attribution capture on the enquiry form.**

## Their weaknesses

- **Residential-focused.** The page covers first home, refinance and investment. No commercial,
  business or SMSF offer — which is where The Loans Suite's actual range sits.
- **Franchise, not independent.** The Loans Suite holds a **national Independent Office of the
  Year win**, a category a franchise is structurally ineligible for. That is the sharpest
  available contrast and it never has to name anyone.
- **The brand is the relationship, not the person.** Loan Market's promises are network-level.
- They use **"tailored solutions"** — exactly the exhausted category vocabulary in
  [banned-language](../04-voice-and-messaging/banned-language.md). Do not follow them there.

## What to take from this

1. **Stop leading with panel size in Moreton Bay.** Lead with **range** — residential *and*
   commercial *and* asset *and* SMSF under one principal — which they cannot match, and with
   **independence**, which they structurally cannot claim.
2. **Add attribution capture** to the contact form. "How did you find us?" is one field and it
   tells you which channel is actually working.
3. **The ongoing-review promise needs to be on the page.** The Loans Suite already *does* this —
   Pipeline 6 runs a month-10 rate health check — and Loan Market says it out loud while The
   Loans Suite keeps it internal. See [11-operations/pipelines](../11-operations/pipelines.md).
