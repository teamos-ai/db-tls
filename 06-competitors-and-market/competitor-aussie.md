# Aussie

**Type:** National franchise brokerage, part of Lendi Group. The category's most recognised
consumer brand.

## What they publish, verbatim

> Our mission is to help Aussies reach their property goals faster. We can compare thousands
> of home loans from over 25 leading lenders.

> Your current bank isn't your only option — one of our 25+ lenders could offer you a better deal.

> Help from an Aussie Broker comes at no cost to you.
> *(footnoted: "Individual lenders may charge fees to the customer")*

> We do the heavy lifting and work around your schedule.

## What they do well

- **Brand recognition.** Decades of advertising. Most Australians can name Aussie and cannot
  name a second broker.
- **The "your bank isn't your only option" line** is the cleanest articulation of the broker
  value proposition in the market. It is simple, true and undefendable by a bank.
- **Honest fee footnote.** They caveat that lenders may charge fees. Worth copying — see
  [fee-model-and-economics](../02-offer-and-lending/fee-model-and-economics.md).
- Tooling breadth, including a contract analyser.

## Their structural weakness

- **25+ lenders against The Loans Suite's 60+.** Less than half the panel. For a borrower at
  a serviceability wall, that is the whole difference.
- **Residential-focused.** No meaningful commercial, business or asset finance offer.
- **Franchise model** means the individual broker changes; the brand is the relationship.
- Brand-led rather than person-led — the opposite of The Loans Suite's model.

## What we take from this

**The panel number is a live comparison and we win it.** Never say "more than double Aussie's
panel" outward — but the internal knowledge shapes how confidently we state 60+.

**"Your bank isn't your only option"** is the argument, and we should make our own version of
it rather than a version of "we do the hard work", which they own and which is generic anyway.

**Do not compete on brand recognition.** It is unwinnable and irrelevant at the point of
decision, where a real person's competence matters more than a jingle.
