SETUP CHECKLIST
What to configure in GHL
Pipelines and opportunities
A Lead to Application pipeline with stages matching this process, so every lead's position is visible at a
glance.
Workflows
One workflow per automated step: auto-reply, scoring, nurture drip, fact find send, and document request.
Forms and surveys
Qualification questions and the fact find built as native GHL forms, writing straight to the contact record.
Calendars
Individual broker calendars for Kaiden and Jess, with round robin distribution if leads should split
automatically.
Custom fields and tags
Source, loan type, borrowing capacity range, broker assigned, and a post-settlement tag to key reporting off.
Sales Trekker bridge
A webhook or middleware connection (Zapier or Make) so application data leaves GHL at lodgement, and
settlement data returns to trigger the retention tag.
Broker performance fields
Broker owner, settlement amount, settlement date and commission tier as custom fields, so every settled loan
rolls up to the right broker automatically.
Commission workflow
A workflow that applies the tiered commission structure the moment a loan is marked settled, so figures are
consistent and ready for payroll without manual calculation.
KPI reporting view
A broker-filtered report or dashboard inside GHL for settlement volume, new clients and commission, kept in
sync with the monday.com Broker KPI board for the activity-based metrics.
