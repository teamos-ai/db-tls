Compare home loans before you choose

Home loans can vary significantly between lenders. Interest rates, fees, repayment options, offset accounts, redraw facilities, fixed and variable structures, and eligibility requirements can all affect the overall value of a loan.

The Loans Suite Australia helps clients in Sydney, Brisbane and across Australia compare home loan options from a wide panel of lenders. Whether you are buying your first home, refinancing, upgrading, investing or building, we can help you understand the differences between loans and what they may mean for you.

Why home loan comparison matters

A small difference in rate or loan structure can make a meaningful difference over time. However, the cheapest rate is not always the most suitable option if the loan does not have the features, flexibility or lender policy you need.

When comparing home loans, it is worth looking at:

Interest rates and comparison rates

Upfront and ongoing fees

Fixed, variable and split rate options

Offset account availability

Redraw options

Extra repayment flexibility

Loan term and repayment type

Lender policy and approval requirements

Cashback or refinance offers where available

How the loan fits your long-term plans

A comparison tool can help you narrow your options, but it does not replace personalised lending guidance.

Home loan comparison in Sydney, Brisbane and across Australia

The Loans Suite Australia supports borrowers in Sydney, Brisbane and Australia-wide. Property markets, purchase prices and borrowing needs can vary from state to state, so it helps to compare loans in the context of your personal situation.

A first home buyer in Sydney may need a different loan structure from a family upgrading in Brisbane or an investor buying interstate. Some borrowers want repayment flexibility, while others may value certainty, offset features or a sharper refinance rate.

Our role is to help you compare the details, not just the headline rate.
