What You Need to Know About Construction Loans

Construction loans are assessed differently to standard home loans, and the details of your build can affect your approval, valuation and repayments.

Lenders will usually need a fixed-price building contract, plans and council approvals.

Funds are generally released in stages rather than as one lump sum.

Changes to the build, budget or timeline may affect your loan approval or drawdowns.

FAQs About Construction Loans

How does a construction loan work?

A construction loan usually releases funds in stages as each part of the build is completed. These stages may include slab, frame, lock-up, fixing and completion, depending on the lender and builder contract.

Do I need a fixed-price building contract?

Most lenders prefer or require a fixed-price building contract because it helps confirm the cost of the project. Some lenders may consider other arrangements, but requirements can vary.

Do I pay interest on the full loan amount straight away?

Usually, interest is charged only on the amount drawn down during construction. This means repayments may increase as more funds are released throughout the build.
