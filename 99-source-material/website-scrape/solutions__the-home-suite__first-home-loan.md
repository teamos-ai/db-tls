Grants and incentives many first home buyers miss

Australian Government 5% Deposit Scheme

Fast-track your path to home ownership with the Australian Government 5% Deposit Scheme. The Scheme is designed for aspiring home buyers just like you – making home ownership more achievable by helping you buy sooner with a small deposit.

First Home Super Saver Scheme

You can make voluntary contributions up to $15,000 per year and $50,000 in total to your super (on top of what your employer already pays) – either before tax through salary sacrifice or after tax as personal contributions.

Australian Government Help to Buy Scheme

Limited information available on this one! The Help to Buy Scheme is a new initiative designed to make home ownership more achievable with shared equity support from the Australian Government. This scheme is not for everyone, Get in touch today to discuss how it may work for you.
