FAQs About Property Investment Loans

Can I use equity to buy an investment property?

Yes, many property investors use existing equity as part of their deposit or purchase strategy. The amount available will depend on your property value, current loan balance, income, expenses and lender requirements.

Do lenders include rental income when assessing my loan?

In many cases, yes. Lenders may include expected or existing rental income, but they often assess it at a reduced percentage to allow for vacancies, costs and risk.

Should I choose principal and interest or interest-only repayments?

Both options can suit different strategies. Principal and interest repayments help reduce the loan balance over time, while interest-only repayments may help with short-term cash flow, depending on your goals and circumstances.
