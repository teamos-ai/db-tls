What You Need to Know About Commercial Finance

Commercial loans are often assessed differently to standard home loans or smaller business loans, so the right preparation can make a real difference.

Lenders may consider business income, leases, security, trading history and financial statements.

Loan terms, interest rates and repayment structures can vary widely.

Some commercial finance options may require a larger deposit or lower loan-to-value ratio.

FAQs About Commercial Finance

What can commercial finance be used for?

Commercial finance can be used to purchase commercial property, refinance existing business or property debt, fund expansion, support cash flow, buy equipment or complete business premises fit-outs.

Is commercial finance only for property purchases?

No. Commercial finance can include property lending, business lending, asset finance, working capital facilities and other funding options depending on the business need.

What documents do lenders usually need?

Requirements vary, but lenders may ask for financial statements, tax returns, BAS, bank statements, lease agreements, business plans, property details and information about existing debts.
