Karlie Scharfenberg

Commercial & Residential Broker

Karlie began her journey with The Loans Suite in 2016, building on her successful banking career to become an award-winning Senior Finance Broker. Renowned for her expertise, Karlie is dedicated to helping families and individuals achieve their financial goals. Whether assisting first-time homebuyers, securing favorable bank deals, or guiding property investments, she is committed to providing personalised, people-centered service.

As a “Financial Architect,” Karlie’s mission is to empower everyday Australians to dream big, pay off their family homes, and leverage property investments for passive income, ensuring a secure financial future.

With a Cert IV & Diploma in Finance & Mortgage Broking Management, over 20 years of experience, and her role as an NSW Justice of the Peace, Karlie specializes in Residential and Commercial Lending, Car & Equipment Finance, and Self-Managed Super Fund Lending. Her tailored approach ensures clients receive expert guidance and support to achieve financial success.

Karlie is passionate about traveling, experiencing new adventures, and helping clients realise their financial dreams and goals.
