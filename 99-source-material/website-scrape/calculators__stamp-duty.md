How stamp duty works and why it matters when buying property

Buying property comes with more than just the purchase price. Stamp duty, also known as transfer duty, can be one of the largest upfront costs you need to plan for when buying a home, investment property or land in Australia.

Use our stamp duty calculator to estimate how much duty may apply based on the state or territory you are buying in, the property value, the property type and whether you may be eligible for first home buyer concessions.

Frequently asked questions

What is stamp duty?

Stamp duty, also known as transfer duty, is a government charge that may apply when you buy property in Australia. The amount can vary depending on the property location, purchase price, property type and your eligibility for concessions.

Is stamp duty the same in every state?

No. Each state and territory has its own stamp duty rules, rates, thresholds and concessions. This means the cost can vary significantly between NSW, Queensland, Victoria, Western Australia, South Australia, Tasmania, the ACT and the Northern Territory.

Do first home buyers pay stamp duty?

Some first home buyers may be eligible for stamp duty concessions or exemptions, depending on the state or territory they are buying in, the property value and their personal circumstances. It is important to check the current rules before relying on an estimate.

Can stamp duty be added to my home loan?

In many cases, stamp duty needs to be paid from your own funds as part of settlement costs. Some buyers may be able to structure their lending differently depending on available equity, deposit size and lender policy. A mortgage broker can help you understand what may be possible.

Should I calculate stamp duty before applying for a home loan?

Yes. Estimating stamp duty early can help you understand your full buying costs and avoid surprises when applying for finance. It can also help you work out whether you have enough deposit and funds available to complete the purchase.

Can The Loans Suite Australia help if I am buying interstate?

Yes. The Loans Suite Australia has offices in Sydney and Brisbane and services clients across Australia. We can help you understand your lending options whether you are buying locally, interstate or investing in another property market.
