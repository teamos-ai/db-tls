Kaiden Harrison

QLD Residential Broker

Kaiden Harrison is proudly stepping into the world of mortgage broking, backed by a lifetime of insight and two years of hands-on experience behind the scenes at The Loans Suite.

Mentored by his mother, Karlie Scharfenberg—an industry powerhouse with over 30 years of expertise—Kaiden has grown up surrounded by conversations about home loans, exceptional customer service, and the importance of finding the right financial solutions for every client.

While most teens were studying for exams, Kaiden was also completing his Certificate IV in Finance and Mortgage Broking during Year 12, setting a strong foundation for his career. He’s spent the last two years immersed in auditing files and mastering the intricate processes behind the scenes, giving him a solid understanding of what makes a great loan—and an even greater client experience.

With fresh energy, sharp attention to detail, and the guidance of one of the industry’s best, Kaiden is ready to help the next generation of homeowners and investors achieve their property goals.
