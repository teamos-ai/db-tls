What is home equity?

Your home equity can open up options for your next financial move. Whether you are thinking about renovating, investing, refinancing or consolidating debts, understanding how much equity you may have is a useful place to start.

Use our equity calculator to estimate the difference between your property’s current value and the amount still owing on your home loan.

How is equity calculated?

Home equity is the portion of your property you effectively own. It is generally calculated by subtracting your remaining loan balance from your estimated property value.

For example, if your home is worth $900,000 and your loan balance is $500,000, your equity is approximately $400,000.

What is usable equity?

Usable equity is the amount of equity a lender may allow you to access, subject to their lending criteria and your ability to repay the loan.

You may not be able to use all of your equity. Lenders will usually consider factors such as your property value, loan balance, income, expenses, credit history and overall borrowing position before approving additional lending.

How could equity be used?

Depending on your circumstances and lender approval, equity may be used to help with:

Buying an investment property

Renovating or improving your home

Refinancing your current loan

Consolidating debts

Funding major personal expenses

Reviewing your overall loan structure

Planning your next property purchase

An equity calculator gives you an estimate only. The amount you may be able to access can depend on your property value, income, loan balance, lender policy and broader financial position.

Frequently asked questions

What is home equity?

Home equity is the difference between your property’s estimated value and the amount owing on your home loan.

How do I calculate equity?

You can estimate equity by subtracting your current loan balance from your property’s estimated market value.

Can I use equity to buy another property?

You may be able to use usable equity from your current property to help fund the deposit or purchase costs for another property, subject to lender approval.

Can I use equity for renovations?

Yes, some borrowers use equity to fund renovations or home improvements. The amount available will depend on your property value, loan balance, income and lender criteria.

Does an equity calculator confirm how much I can borrow?

No. An equity calculator provides an estimate only. Your borrowing position will depend on your income, expenses, debts, credit profile, property value and lender requirements.

Can The Loans Suite Australia help me review my equity?
