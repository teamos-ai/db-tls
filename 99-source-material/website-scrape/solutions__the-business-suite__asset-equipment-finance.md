What You Need to Know About Asset Finance

Asset and equipment finance can vary depending on the asset, business structure, loan type and lender requirements.

The age, value and type of asset can affect your finance options.

Some lenders may require financials, BAS, tax returns or business bank statements.

Loan terms, balloon payments and ownership structures can change the overall cost.

FAQs About Asset and Equipment Finance

What can asset finance be used for?

Asset finance can be used for a wide range of business assets, including vehicles, machinery, equipment, tools, technology and other items used to operate or grow a business.

Do I need to provide full financials?

Not always. Some lenders may offer low-doc or alternative verification options, depending on the business, asset, loan amount and overall application strength.

Can I finance used equipment?

Yes, used equipment may be eligible for finance, but lenders will usually consider the asset’s age, condition, value and expected useful life.
