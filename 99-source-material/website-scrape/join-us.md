Join The Loans Suite

Thinking about a career in broking? We're always happy to chat.

The Loans Suite is an established, high performing brokerage built on strategy, results and genuine client relationships. Now we're looking for driven brokers ready to build their own business under a brand that already has the runs on the board.

Let's talk

What we offer

We offer sub broker arrangements with a commission split structure, giving you the freedom to generate your own leads and grow your own business, backed by the systems, support and reputation of an established brand.

You'll get access to our marketing team, operational support and a close knit team environment, along with the infrastructure and processes we've spent years refining. You're not starting from scratch. You're building on a strong foundation.

Are you a single operator or sick of going it alone?

Running your own show can be rewarding, but it can also be lonely. If you're a single operator broker who is over doing everything yourself with no support, no team and no one to bounce ideas off, come and join us.

You keep your independence and your own business, but you gain a team, a brand, and the backing to help you grow further and faster than you could on your own.

Who we're looking for

We're after brokers with at least two years of brokerage or banking experience, and the hustle and drive to grow their own business.

If you're ambitious, client focused and ready to take ownership of your own success, we want to hear from you.

Ready to talk?

If broking under a brand that backs you sounds like the next step, get in touch. Let's have a chat about what building your business with The Loans Suite could look like.
