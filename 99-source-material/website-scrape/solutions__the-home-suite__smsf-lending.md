What You Need to Know About SMSF Lending

SMSF lending is highly specialised, and the rules can affect what you can buy, how the loan is structured and whether borrowing is available at all.

New SMSF borrowing for residential property is changing from August 2026.

Commercial property may still be an option for eligible SMSFs.

You will generally need advice from an accountant, financial adviser or SMSF specialist before proceeding.

FAQs About SMSF Loans

Can an SMSF still borrow to buy residential property?

New SMSF borrowing for residential property is being restricted from 10 August 2026. Existing arrangements may not be affected, but anyone considering a purchase should get advice before taking action.

Can an SMSF borrow to buy commercial property?

SMSF borrowing for commercial property may still be available, subject to the fund, the property, lender requirements and the correct SMSF loan structure.

Do I need advice before applying for an SMSF loan?

Yes. SMSF lending involves superannuation law, tax, investment strategy and lending requirements, so you should speak with qualified SMSF, tax and financial advice professionals before applying.
