What You Need to Know About Business Loans

Business finance can vary depending on your trading history, revenue, purpose, security and the lender’s appetite.

Some loans may be secured, while others may be unsecured.

Lenders may assess cash flow, trading history and existing debts.

Loan terms, fees and repayment frequency can affect your business cash flow.

FAQs About Business Loans

What can a business loan be used for?

A business loan can be used for working capital, growth, stock, equipment, vehicles, fit-outs, refinancing, marketing or other business-related expenses.

Do I need security for a business loan?

Not always. Some lenders offer unsecured business loans, while others may require residential property, commercial property, business assets or other forms of security.

How quickly can business finance be approved?

Approval timeframes vary depending on the lender, loan type, application strength and documents required. Some business loans can move in as little as a few hours, while more complex applications may take longer.
