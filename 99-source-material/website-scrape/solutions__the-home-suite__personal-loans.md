Eligibility Criteria

You must be 18 years or older and an Australian citizen, Permanent Resident or New Zealand citizen.

You need to have photo identification documents such as your valid Australian passport or driver’s to licence, to hand.

You must have paid employment (which may include self-employment) and ideally your credit file won't have any defaults, judgments or bankruptcies.

You need to borrow at least $2,000 with your loan.
