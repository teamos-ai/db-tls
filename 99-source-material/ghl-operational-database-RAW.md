## 2. The 6 Core Pipelines (Salestrekker & GHL Aligned)

These pipelines represent the exact operational workflow extracted from Karlie's production systems, mapped to automated GHL triggers:

### Pipeline 1: 1. Leads & Inbound Qualification
* **Purpose:** Triage raw inbound web inquiries, Meta ad leads, and referral partner introductions within 5 minutes.
* **Stages:**
  1. `New Lead Set up` — Automated missed-call text-back & instant SMS qualification sequence triggered.
  2. `Contact Made / Discovery Booked` — Initial 15-minute qualification call scheduled.
  3. `Credit Guide Sent` — Mandatory Credit Guide dispatched via SMS/Email portal.
  4. `Credit Guide Signed / Fact Find Issued` — Digital Fact Find and 100-point ID link sent.
  5. `On Hold / Nurture` — Unserviceable or long-term deposit saver sequence active.

### Pipeline 2: 2. Loan Submission & Strategy Formulation
* **Purpose:** Managing file packaging, living expense scrubbing, and team-member allocation.
* **Stages:**
  1. `Jess Clients` — Assigned to senior broker/processor Jessica Didovich.
  2. `Kaiden Clients` — Assigned to broker Kaiden Harrison.
  3. `Karlie Clients` — Retained by Principal Karlie Scharfenberg (VIP/High-Net-Worth/Complex Commercial).
  4. `Reema Files` — Dedicated packaging desk.
  5. `The Shitshow` — Stalled/complex credit files requiring policy exceptions or BDM escalation.
  6. `Prelim Pre / AOL Setup` — Serviceability calculated (Quickli), living expenses reconciled, ApplyOnline draft initiated.
  7. `Karlie Mentor Check` — Quality assurance sign-off by Principal prior to submission.
  8. `AOL Submission` — File formally submitted through ApplyOnline to lender.

### Pipeline 3: 3. Approval & Settlement Engine
* **Purpose:** Managing the file through lender queues, credit assessment, valuation, and PEXA settlement.
* **Stages:**
  1. `Submitted / In Queue` — File lodged; automated SMS sent to client: *"Your application is in the queue with [Lender]"*.
  2. `MIR's (More Information Required)` — Assessor requested clarification; high-priority task created for processor.
  3. `Approved - Pending (Conditional / AIP)` — Valuation ordered; property inspection tracked.
  4. `Formal Approval (Unconditional)` — Loan unconditionally approved; celebration SMS & conveyancer update sent.
  5. `Loan Docs Issued / Signed` — Mortgage contract executed with lender.
  6. `Settlement Booked` — PEXA workspace open; funds-to-complete shortfall verified.
  7. `Settled` — Drawdown complete; settlement celebration gift dispatched.
  8. `Audit & Compliance Check` — Final BID file note archived for aggregator compliance.
  9. `30 Day Calls` — First repayment check-in scheduled.
  10. `HOLD / NPW (Not Proceeded With)` — File withdrawn or expired.

### Pipeline 4: 4. Pre-Approval Lifecycle
* **Purpose:** Tracking house hunters during their 90-day pre-approval property search window.
* **Stages:**
  1. `Pre-Approval Active` — Pre-approval granted by lender.
  2. `30 Days Check-in` — Automated SMS: *"How is the property search going? Any contracts of sale to review?"*
  3. `60 Days Check-in` — Property report offer / auction guide dispatched.
  4. `75 Days Expiry Warning` — Warning that pay slips must be refreshed before the 90-day mark.
  5. `Expired / Extension Requested` — Re-submitting updated bank statements and pay slips.

### Pipeline 5: 6. Construction Loans Workflow
* **Purpose:** Managing staged drawdown payments for building contracts.
* **Stages:**
  1. `Settled Construction Not Started` — Land settled; builder slab preparation.
  2. `Deposit / Pool Stage` — Initial builder deposit claim verified.
  3. `Progress Claim 1 - Base` — Slab poured, inspection sign-off.
  4. `Progress Claim 2 - Frame` — Wall and roof framing complete.
  5. `Progress Claim 3 - Enclosed` — Lock-up stage reached.
  6. `Progress Claim 4 - Fixing` — Internal plastering, cabinetry, and tiling.
  7. `Progress Claim 5 - Practical Completion` — Final handover inspection.
  8. `Landscaping Payments` — Out-of-contract additions.
  9. `Final Hand Over` — Final occupancy certificate received.
  10. `Repricing / Post-Build Valuation` — Re-valuing completed home to reduce LVR.

### Pipeline 6: 7. Client Retention & Refinance Shield
* **Purpose:** Protecting the $50M+ trail book against bank retention teams and 24-month clawbacks.
* **Stages:**
  1. `Month 6 Check-in` — Offset account optimization check.
  2. `Month 10 Rate Health Check` — Automated lender pricing request submitted to match front-book discounts.
  3. `Month 18 Equity Review` — CoreLogic/RPData automated equity report sent to borrower.
  4. `Month 23 Refinance Window Open` — Clawback period expired; full market comparison offered.

## 3. Marketing Strategies & Funnel Architecture

### Funnel 1: The "Mortgage Stress & Rate Check" Funnel
* **Traffic Sources:** Google Local Search Ads, Meta Ads ("Mortgage Repayment Reality Check"), Organic SEO.
* **Mechanism:** 4-step micro-commitment form asking:
  1. What is your current estimated property value?
  2. What is your approximate remaining loan balance?
  3. What is your current interest rate? (Dropdown: <5.8%, 5.8%–6.4%, >6.4%, Not sure).
  4. Where should we send your custom Rate Savings Report? (Name, Email, Mobile).
* **Automated Action:** Instantly calculates estimated annual interest savings, sends a dynamic SMS report, and opens a calendar booking slot for a 15-minute Strategy Call with Karlie or Jess.

### Funnel 2: The B2B Professional Referrer Portal (Accountants, REAs, Conveyancers)
* **Target:** Belle Property Newport, Ray White Redcliffe, Towns Conveyancing, Clear Credit Solutions, Accurate Account Solutions.
* **Mechanism:** Dedicated co-branded landing pages (`theloanssuite.com.au/partners/[partner-name]`).
* **Feature:** When a partner submits a client lead, GHL automatically:
  1. Tags the lead with the partner's unique Partner ID.
  2. Sends an automated webhook notification to the partner when milestones occur (e.g., *"John's loan formally approved with Macquarie"*).
  3. Preserves client Privacy Act confidentiality while keeping the referrer 100% informed.

## 6. Live Chat Widget & 24/7 AI Voice Agent Architecture

### 1. GHL Omnichannel Chat Widget
* **Placement:** Bottom-right corner of `theloanssuite.com.au`.
* **Design:** Champagne gold pill badge `#D1BCA0` with avatar of Karlie Scharfenberg.
* **Greeting:** *"Hi there! Looking to check your borrowing capacity or review your current home loan rate? Ask me anything or request a call."*
* **Lead Capture:** Asks 2 qualification questions (Purchase vs Refinance + Contact Number) before handing off to live SMS.

### 2. Conversational AI Voice Agent ("Sophie — The Loan Suite Specialist")
* **Role:** Answering inbound calls during client consultations, after-hours (after 5:30 PM AEST), and on weekends.
* **Voice Profile:** Warm, professional, Australian accent, articulate, calm.
* **Core Scripting & Intent Mapping:**
  - *Intent A: New Loan Enquiry:* Identifies purchase vs. refinance, approximate budget, and books a discovery call directly onto the GHL calendar.
  - *Intent B: Existing Client Status Check:* Asks for client name, checks GHL pipeline stage, and reassures: *"Your application is currently under formal assessment with the lender's credit team; Jess will send your daily update at 4:00 PM."*
  - *Intent C: Urgent Settlement / Finance Clause Issue:* Instantly alerts Karlie via high-priority push notification and SMS.
* **Strict Guardrails:** The AI voice agent *never* provides specific credit advice, quotes interest rates as guaranteed, or commits to loan approval amounts. It strictly qualifies and schedules.
