# Banned language

Three lists. The first is a compliance matter and is absolute. The second and third are
positioning matters and should be treated as near-absolute — they are what stops generated
copy sounding like every other broker.

## 1. Regulatory weight — never, in any channel

**approved · pre-approved · guaranteed · guarantee · you qualify · best rate · lowest rate ·
cheapest · we advise · our advice · instant approval · risk-free · we'll save you $X ·
act now before rates rise**

Full reasoning and replacements in
[guardrails](../07-compliance-and-guardrails/guardrails.md). That file wins over this one.

## 2. Exhausted by the category — do not use

Every one of these appears on multiple competitor sites. Using them makes The Loans Suite
sound interchangeable with the brokerage next door.

| Banned | Why | Use instead |
|---|---|---|
| **tailored** / **bespoke** | Universal in broker copy. The site uses "tailor" twice; stop there. | "built around", "structured for", or just describe what actually differs |
| **solutions** | The emptiest word in financial services. | Name the thing: "a loan", "finance", "an option" |
| **journey** | Exhausted across all of marketing. | "process", "what happens next", or nothing |
| **seamless** / **hassle-free** | Claims made by everyone, believed by no one. | Say what you do: "we handle the paperwork and liaise with the lender" |
| **leverage** (as a verb) | Corporate. In lending it is also confusable with actual leverage. | "use" |
| **empower** | Hollow. | "help you", or describe the outcome |
| **unlock** | Especially "unlock your equity" — near-universal. | "access", "release", "use" |
| **partner with us** / **your finance partner** | Generic B2B filler. | Say what the relationship does |
| **cutting-edge** / **innovative** / **game-changing** | Nothing in mortgage broking is any of these. | omit |
| **passionate about** | Every About page in Australia. | Show it instead |
| **at the end of the day** / **when it comes to** | Filler. | Delete; the sentence is better without |
| **in today's market** | Dated the moment it is written. | Name the actual condition and date it |

## 3. Off-brand for this business specifically

| Banned | Why |
|---|---|
| **boutique** | The design is boutique; the language must not be. Saying it undoes it. Belle Property's partner page uses it — let them. |
| **"we work for you, not the banks"** | Category cliché that also raises an awkward question, given lenders pay the commission. The site never says it. |
| **"the big four are ripping you off"** | Adversarial framing that sits badly against best interests duty. Compare structurally, never attack. |
| **Naming a competitor** | Never, outward-facing. [06-competitors-and-market](../06-competitors-and-market/README.md) shapes *how we say things*, never *who we attack*. |
| **Emoji stacking / all-caps / multiple exclamation marks** | The visual identity is restrained. The copy should match. One exclamation mark, occasionally, is the ceiling. |
| **"The Loan Suite"** (singular) | Wrong name. See [company-profile](../01-company/company-profile.md). |
| **American spelling** | favorable, specializes, utilizing, personalized. Australian English throughout. |

## 4. Handle with care — not banned, but earn them

- **"Financial architects"** — the brand's own and genuinely differentiated. **Use it
  sparingly.** Its power comes from being the one elevated phrase in plain copy. Diluted
  across every headline, it becomes a slogan.
- **"Strategy" / "strategic"** — accurate to the positioning, but three uses in one page turns
  it into noise. Once per asset.
- **"Wealth"** — fine in the brand's aspirational register (*"build long-term wealth through
  smart property investing"*). Never in a predictive one.
- **"Fast"** — safe applied to an *application* or a *conversation*. Never to an **approval**.

## The check before shipping

Search the draft for: *tailored, bespoke, solutions, seamless, journey, unlock, empower,
leverage, boutique, approved, guaranteed, best rate*. If any appear, rewrite that sentence.
