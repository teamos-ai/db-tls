# Positioning

## The statement

**For Australians whose finance is more complicated than a rate comparison — investors
building a portfolio, business owners funding growth, families buying their first or next
home — The Loans Suite structures the loan around what you're actually trying to do, across
more than sixty lenders, at no cost to you. Because the rate is the part everyone compares
and the part that matters least.**

## The central metaphor

**"Your financial architects."**

It is the strongest brand asset the business owns, and it is doing real work rather than
decorating:

- An architect **designs the structure**; a builder just quotes a price. That is precisely
  the rate-versus-structure argument, in one word.
- It implies **planning ahead** — the loan you take now determines what you can do in three
  years.
- It justifies the premium visual identity without the copy having to sound premium.
- Nobody else in the category uses it.

The homepage lands it plainly: *"This is why we call ourselves financial architects."*

**Use it sparingly.** It works because it is the one elevated phrase in otherwise plain copy.

## The competitive frame

The Loans Suite competes against three structurally different things, and needs a different
argument for each. It should never compete on the same axis as any of them.

| Competitor type | Their strength | Their structural weakness | Our line |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The bank branch** | Trust, incumbency, one relationship | **One lender.** They can only offer you their own product | "Your bank has one answer. We have sixty." |
| **Comparison sites / digital brokers** (Lendi, comparison portals) | Free, instant, no human friction | **You do the work, and nobody fights for you when it goes wrong** | "Saves you time and money by avoiding endless comparison sites. We do the legwork." |
| **Franchise brokers** (Aussie, Loan Market, Mortgage Choice) | Brand recognition, national footprint. **Loan Market's local office claims 100+ lenders — more than us** | **Residential-only** in most cases, and structurally ineligible for an *Independent* Office of the Year award | Independence, and the full finance range under one principal — not panel size |

## What is genuinely defensible

1. **Product range, not panel count.** More than 60 lenders across residential, commercial,
   business, asset and SMSF finance. The count beats Aussie (25+) and Borro (30+) but **loses to
   the local Loan Market franchise, which claims 100+** — so lead with the *range of finance
   types*, which no local residential-only competitor offers, not the lender number.
   Corrected 2026-08-27; see [competitor-loan-market-aqua](../06-competitors-and-market/competitor-loan-market-aqua.md).
2. **Commercial plus residential under one principal.** Karlie's published title is
   *Commercial & Residential Broker*. Most residential brokers cannot do commercial; most
   commercial brokers won't do the family home. A business owner does not need two people.
3. **A national win as Independent Office of the Year (2023), plus seven shortlistings** across
   two bodies. The word *Independent* is the point — a franchise cannot win that category.
4. **A published referral ecosystem of twelve partners** across the four professions a
   property transaction touches. Almost no competitor publishes one.
5. **The rescue story.** Samantha G's testimonial is a concrete instance of the thing a
   comparison site structurally cannot do.

## What is not defensible — and should not be claimed

- **"Personalised service."** Every broker says it. Undifferentiated.
- **"Access to a wide panel."** True but commoditised; the number is what matters, not the claim.
- **"We take the time to understand you."** Category wallpaper.
- **"Local."** Two offices, but the copy also claims national. Pick one per asset.
- **Any experience or volume figure.** Unverified. See
  [what-we-cannot-claim](../05-proof-and-evidence/what-we-cannot-claim.md).

## The positioning risk to manage

**The visual identity and the verbal identity are pulling in different directions.** The
design is dark, editorial and expensive — Canela Thin at 80px on espresso and champagne. The
copy is warm, plain and suburban — *"Hate maths? Lets us help you with that."*

That gap is not necessarily a flaw; premium visuals plus accessible language is a legitimate
and disarming combination. **But it must be deliberate.** The failure mode is copy that drifts
upward to match the design and becomes the generic "boutique bespoke tailored" register the
[banned-language](banned-language.md) file exists to prevent.

**Rule: let the design carry the premium. Keep the language human.**
